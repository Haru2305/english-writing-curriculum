# 表現集

全教材のモデル答案(156教材)から、どのテーマでも使える表現を選びました。
「使用」は、その表現がモデル答案に出てくる教材の数です。**補充**は、モデル答案には出てこないが入試で使う表現です。

## 答える・主張する

### X should (not) V because Z.  (使用 24/156教材)

主張と理由を一文で示す

> The university should pilot Option B because earlier engagement is the strongest criterion.  (E102)
>
> A city should not always allocate exactly the same amount of funding to every library because local needs can differ.  (E024)

- 注意: should not always のように always を外すと『全部だめ』と言い過ぎない。because の後ろは主張を支える基準や事実にする

### I suggest that S V(原形).  (使用 6/156教材)

提案を述べる

> I suggest that schools lend devices to students who cannot afford them.  (E003)
>
> I suggest that policy makers improve staffing support as well as physical facilities.  (E006)

- 注意: suggest that の後ろは動詞の原形(または should + 原形)。suggest + 人 + to V の形は使えない

### X should V only if ...  (使用 3/156教材)

条件付きで主張する

> Experts should recommend the policy only if the expected benefits remain strong after fairness and practical limits are considered.  (E039)

- 注意: only if は『その場合だけ』と強く限定する。条件を満たさないときは反対と言い切ることになるので、主張の範囲に合うか確認する

## 主張の強さを調整する

### A can / may V  (使用 143/156教材)

起こり得る・かもしれない(断定を避ける)

> One country can reduce domestic emissions, but pollution may still arrive from neighboring regions.  (E019)
>
> Telemedicine may lead to better access by reducing the need for long trips.  (E002)

- 注意: can は『起こり得る・できる』、may は『かもしれない』。will・always・must にすると本文以上の強さになりやすい

### A does not show / prove that B.  (使用 11/156教材)

根拠が支える範囲を超えない

> A quiet period does not prove that preparedness is unnecessary.  (E011)
>
> A signed form alone does not show that the person understood the study or made a voluntary choice.  (E030)

- 注意: 強さは suggest < show < prove の順。prove は強い語なので、否定形で『言い過ぎ』を避けるのに使う

### A suggests that B.  (使用 20/156教材)

結果が示唆する(まだ断定しない)

> First-month results suggest that discounts can lower waste, but the effect varies by location, with Store C still leaving more food unsold.  (E100)
>
> The trial suggests that students value the homework-help session before major exams, but it does not show that demand will remain high during ordinary weeks.  (E086)

- 注意: suggest には2つの意味がある。主語がデータ・研究(results / trial)なら『示唆する』、人(I suggest that ...)なら『提案する』。提案の用法は動詞の原形を使うので、上の I suggest that の項目も参照

### A shows that B.  (使用 19/156教材)

資料・研究がはっきり示すことを述べる

> Text B shows that a formal leave policy is insufficient if employees are afraid to use it.  (E048)
>
> Text B shows that one visible source can produce a poor conclusion when important groups are missing from it.  (E054)

- 注意: show は suggest より強い。主語が資料(table / text / study)のときに使い、結果が限定的なら suggest にする

### A depends on B.  (使用 18/156教材)

結果が条件や要因に左右される

> Regional access depends on several factors, so increasing staff alone may not solve the whole problem.  (E001)
>
> However, its benefits depend on reliable technology and patients' ability to use it.  (E002)

- 注意: depend on の形で使う(×depend of / from)。要因が複数あるなら factors と複数形にする

### what is known / what remains uncertain  (使用 22/156教材)

分かっていることと分からないことを分ける

> An effective warning should communicate what is known, what remains uncertain, and what residents should do.  (E023)

- 注意: uncertain は『まだ分からない点』。『間違い』『問題点』とは別の意味。分からない点は、必要な根拠まで書くと答案が閉じる

## 理由・結果をつなぐ

### ..., so ...  (使用 33/156教材)

原因から結果・提案へつなぐ

> Regional access depends on several factors, so increasing staff alone may not solve the whole problem.  (E001)
>
> It may also place more inconvenience on households with less flexibility, so efficiency and distribution should be evaluated separately.  (E117)

- 注意: so の前が理由、後ろが結果や提案。文頭に So を置くのは避ける

### X therefore V ...  (使用 50/156教材)

前の文を理由として結論を述べる

> The university should therefore track both the number of new participants and the proportion who first use support before the final two weeks.  (E102)
>
> The appropriate margin therefore depends on conditions such as forecast accuracy, demand variability, and backup reliability.  (E156)

- 注意: 前の文が理由になっているときだけ使う。位置は文頭(Therefore,)か、主語の後・助動詞の後

### A may lead to B.  (使用 7/156教材)

原因から結果を示す(断定しない)

> Telemedicine may lead to better access by reducing the need for long trips.  (E002)
>
> Investment in backup systems may lead to faster recovery when a crisis eventually occurs.  (E011)

- 注意: lead to の後ろは結果を表す名詞。may と一緒に使うと『必ずそうなる』という言い過ぎを避けられる

### A can help (to) V / allow B  (使用 32/156教材)

役に立つ・可能にする

> This can help students who cannot join at a fixed time.  (E003)
>
> A comparison group chosen by the same rule, or repeated measurements before treatment, can help distinguish ordinary variation from a true intervention effect.  (E163)

- 注意: help は help + 人 または help + 動詞原形。allow は allow + 名詞(または allow + 人 + to V)

### As a result, ...  (補充)

前の原因による結果を述べる(補充)

> As a result, fewer students used the library on weekends.

- 注意: 前の文に明確な原因があるときに使う。証拠がまだないときは、As a result ではなく may lead to を使う

## 限定・譲歩・対比する

### A matters, but B ...  (使用 63/156教材)

認めたうえで、本題を後ろに置く

> Individual effort matters, but students do not always study under the same conditions.  (E004)
>
> One country can reduce domestic emissions, but pollution may still arrive from neighboring regions.  (E019)

- 注意: 前半で認め、主張は but の後に置く。but の後ろが重くなるように書く

### However, ...  (使用 51/156教材)

前の内容に対して、限界や反対を述べる

> However, its benefits depend on reliable technology and patients' ability to use it.  (E002)
>
> However, the table covers only average delay and connection counts, not severe-event frequency or passenger volumes.  (E168)

- 注意: 文頭に置き、後ろにカンマを付ける。前の文との関係(逆・限定)が明確なときだけ使う

### Although A, B.  (使用 14/156教材)

譲歩しながら主張する

> Although Option A is expected to attract more students, the table shows that Option B produces a much higher share of early first contact.  (E102)

- 注意: Although の節と主節の間に but は重ねない(×Although A, but B)。主節に主張を置く

### Even if A, B may still ...  (使用 4/156教材)

条件を認めても残る問題を述べる

> Even if a region attracts more doctors, patients may still face long travel times or limited specialist services.  (E001)

- 注意: Even if は『仮に～だとしても』。後ろの主節に still を入れると『それでも残る』が伝わる

### A is limited / only ...  (使用 23/156教材)

根拠の範囲を限定する

> The platform should continue the label pilot, but only as a limited intervention.  (E119)
>
> However, the table covers only average delay and connection counts, not severe-event frequency or passenger volumes.  (E168)

- 注意: limited は『範囲が狭い』。何が足りないか(何が含まれていないか)を同じ文か次の文で示す

### In contrast, ... / On the other hand, ...  (補充)

二つのものを対比する(補充)

> Urban residents can reach a hospital within minutes. In contrast, rural residents may need to travel for an hour.

- 注意: 対比の例を並べる文で使う。However(前の主張への反論)とは使い分ける

### A unless B / as long as B  (補充)

条件を述べる(補充)

> Remote care cannot replace visits unless patients can use the technology.
>
> A new rule is acceptable as long as exceptions are allowed.

- 注意: unless は if ... not の意味。unless の中に否定語を重ねない(×unless ... not)

## 比較する

### A rather than B  (使用 32/156教材)

Bではなく A を選ぶ・Bではなく A である

> Poor digital access can contribute to continued regional gaps, so remote care should complement rather than replace face-to-face services.  (E002)
>
> First, participation should be voluntary, and wearable data should be treated as a proxy rather than a direct measure of health.  (E214)

- 注意: rather than の前後は同じ形(動詞の原形どうし、名詞どうし)にそろえる

### A, while B ...  (使用 72/156教材)

二つを対比する

> The exam gives comparable evidence across students, while the assignment shows whether knowledge can be applied.  (E216)

- 注意: while は対比の接続詞。A と B は同じ観点で比べる。文頭の While A, B の形も使える

### A improves X but costs Y.  (使用 /156教材)

利点と代償を一文で示す

> It improves reliability but costs money because additional capacity may sit unused much of the time.  (E174)
>
> Full anonymity in system C removes that pressure, but it also weakens accountability because careless comments are difficult to trace.  (E218)

- 注意: 利点だけで終わらず、but の後に対応する代償を一つ具体的に書く。because で理由を足せる

### both A and B  (使用 21/156教材)

両方を並べて示す

> Effective support therefore depends on both policy design and practical access.  (E048)
>
> The university should therefore track both the number of new participants and the proportion who first use support before the final two weeks.  (E102)

- 注意: both A and B の A と B は同じ形(名詞どうしなど)にそろえる

## 具体化・条件・付け足し

### For example, ...  (使用 16/156教材)

具体例を一つ示す

> For example, another leaflet will not help someone whose only available appointment is during a shift they cannot leave.  (E053)
>
> For example, each week students could answer two short questions based on that week's lectures.  (E219)

- 注意: For example の後ろには具体例を一つ。例が複数なら such as を使う

### A such as B, C, and D  (使用 11/156教材)

例を並べて示す

> The appropriate margin therefore depends on conditions such as forecast accuracy, demand variability, and backup reliability.  (E156)

- 注意: such as の後ろは名詞を並べる(例示)。全部を挙げるわけではないので、並べすぎない

### whether ...  (使用 36/156教材)

『かどうか』を確かめる・選ぶ

> Those results would show whether the system improves access without creating a new barrier.  (E101)
>
> Researchers may select people who meet the study criteria, but each person must still choose whether to participate.  (E029)

- 注意: whether は『かどうか』。目的語の位置では if と言い換えられるが、主語・前置詞の後ろでは whether を使う

### If A, B may ...  (使用 43/156教材)

条件を付けて可能性を述べる

> If the investment also reduces fuel risk or emissions, the long-term benefits may justify the initial expense.  (E010)
>
> If everyone sees that one floor will be crowded, many students may move elsewhere and create new congestion.  (E210)

- 注意: if 節の中は現在形にする(will を入れない)。主節は may で可能性にとどめる

### not only A but also B  (補充)

複数の点を足して強調する(補充)

> The policy is not only expensive but also difficult to explain to residents.

- 注意: not only A but also B の A と B は同じ形(形容詞どうしなど)にそろえる

### In addition, ... / Moreover, ...  (補充)

理由や根拠を足す(補充)

> In addition, the program should be evaluated after one year.

- 注意: 原因と結果ではなく『足す』ときの接続語。Therefore / So と混同しない
