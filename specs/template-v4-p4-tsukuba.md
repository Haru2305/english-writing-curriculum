# Template v4 — P4 Tsukuba

## Scope

Applies only to P4 / B035–B039.

Design version: `TPL-v4-P4-TSK`

## Governing principle

Official / current Tsukuba exam conditions override the generic curriculum template.

## Current format basis — checked against actual recent papers

Recent-paper matrix reviewed on 2026-09-30:

### 2023
- Q1 / Q2: long reading with multiple Japanese content-explanation items; answer examples span roughly 24–67 Japanese characters.
- Q3A: three word-order items; answers identify designated word positions.
- Q3B: free composition; published third-party model answer is 83 words.

### 2024
- 120 minutes / 3 major questions.
- Q1: about 980 words; three Japanese explanation items (30 / 50 / 30 characters), cloze, section-heading matching, chronological ordering.
- Q2: about 600 words; Japanese explanation plus cloze-centered comprehension.
- Q3A: three word-order items.
- Q3B: composition based on a short research-related prompt.
- No direct English-to-Japanese translation item; Japanese content explanation dominates.

### 2025
- Q1: predictive-processing passage; several 30–50-character Japanese explanation / summary items plus cloze and choice.
- Q2: history of creativity research; Japanese explanation including a high-load 89-character three-stage summary plus cloze / choice.
- Q3A: three word-order items; designated 3rd / 5th words are answered.
- Q3B: free composition; third-party model answer 82 words.

### 2026
- Q1: passage on whether animals are referred to with “who” or “that”; 50–60-character Japanese explanation, cloze, content-choice.
- Q2: science passage on rogue planets; 40–60-character Japanese explanation, cloze, sentence insertion.
- Q3A: three word-order items, designated word positions.
- Q3B: health challenges affecting the writer’s generation; choose the greatest impact, explain why, and give a concrete example in about 100 words.

### Stable priority inferred from 2023–2026
1. Japanese content explanation under strict character limits.
2. Accurate long-passage structure / reference / contrast tracking.
3. Context-sensitive cloze / heading / ordering / insertion.
4. Word-order construction with precise syntax.
5. Short free composition, generally around 80–100 words, directly answering the prompt with reasons / examples.

Do not over-weight T/F merely because generic trend summaries mention it; recent actual papers show Japanese explanation and contextual selection / cloze as the higher-priority recurring forms.

## Style

- Less teaching commentary than P3.
- The problem-facing first page should resemble an entrance-exam practice sheet.
- Explanations stay after the page break.
- No invented claims about actual Tsukuba past questions inside lesson content.
- Fictional or timeless source passages are preferred for generated practice.

## Timing

B035 and Days 7–8 of B036: 60 minutes.
Days 9–18: 90–120 minutes.
Days 19–30: 120 minutes.
