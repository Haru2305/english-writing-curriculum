# Template v4 — P4 Tsukuba

## Scope

Applies only to P4 / B035–B039.

Design version: `TPL-v4-P4-TSK`

## Governing principle

Official / current Tsukuba exam conditions override the generic curriculum template.

## Current format basis

- 120-minute foreign-language examination in the latest detailed official general-selection schedule.
- No second-stage listening.
- Typical recent structure: 3 major questions.
- Q1 / Q2: long reading, content understanding, Japanese explanation and other mixed response types.
- Q3: integrated reading, word-order / grammar work, and English composition.
- English composition: prepare for 50–100-word range; B035 standard target 80–100 words.
- The detailed 2027 general-selection guidelines must be revalidated before actual learner use.

## B035 rules

Days 1–6, Core 60 minutes each.

- E205: Q1 reading precision
- E206: Q2 reading and Japanese explanation
- E207: Q3A word order / grammar
- E208: Q3B source-linked composition
- E209: reading-to-output timing
- E210: shortened Tsukuba-style checkpoint

## Style

- Less teaching commentary than P3.
- The problem-facing first page should resemble an entrance-exam practice sheet.
- Explanations stay after the page break.
- No invented claims about actual Tsukuba past questions inside lesson content.
- Fictional or timeless source passages are preferred for generated practice.

## Timing

B035 and Days 7–8 of B036: 60 minutes.
Days 9–18: 90–120 minutes.
Days 19–30: 120 minutes.
