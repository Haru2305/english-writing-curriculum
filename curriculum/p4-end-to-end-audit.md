# P4 End-to-End Audit — 2026-10-02

## Scope

This audit treats **E204 → E234** as one continuous exam-preparation sequence.

It checks:
- the handoff from P3 Final Gate to Tsukuba-specific work
- Days 1–8 part drills
- Days 9–18 semi-full progression
- Days 19–30 full-set repetition
- Q1/Q2 Japanese constructed response
- Q3A word-order accuracy
- Q3B free-composition fidelity
- timing and final-review protection
- model-answer and support reduction

No new lesson or exercise is added.

## Final decision

**Keep the 30-day P4 architecture and all E205–E234 lessons.**

The main structure is sound:
- Days 1–8 isolate the recurring Tsukuba formats
- Days 9–12 connect sections and reach the first 120-minute diagnostic
- Days 13–14 perform targeted post-diagnostic repair
- Day 15 onward stabilizes repeated 120-minute full-set execution
- Days 19–30 remain full-set centered

The audit found three repairs worth making, but no reason to expand or reorder the corpus.

## 1. E204 → E205 transition

E204 ends P3 with:
- mixed sources
- English summary
- causal limitation
- conditional recommendation
- 60-minute independent execution

E205 then switches to a Tsukuba Q1-style long passage with strict Japanese character limits.

That shift is appropriate, but it can look like a difficulty drop if the learner sees only the task format. E205 now states explicitly that the P3 skill is being **compressed**, not discarded:

**deep understanding → 30–60-character Japanese explanation under time pressure**

P4 therefore begins by changing the output constraint rather than resetting the intellectual level.

## 2. Workload progression

The intended timing path is:

- Days 1–8: 60 minutes
- Day 9: 90
- Day 10: 100
- Day 11: 110
- Day 12: 120 diagnostic
- Day 13: 100 targeted Q3A repair
- Day 14: 110 targeted Q3B repair
- Days 15–30: 120

The apparent 120 → 100 drop after Day 12 is intentional.

Day 12 is the **first full-format diagnostic exposure**, not the start of uninterrupted full-set repetition. Days 13–14 remove one late section at a time so the learner can repair the specific fatigue weakness exposed by Day 12. Full four-section repetition begins on Day 15.

This logic is now explicit in the P4 plan and B037 README.

## 3. Q3B fidelity correction

P4 should not turn Tsukuba free composition back into a P3 source-integration task.

The audit found ten lessons whose Q3B instructions still explicitly depended on Q1/Q2 reading material:

- E210
- E212
- E213
- E214
- E216
- E218
- E219
- E223
- E225
- E229

These dependencies were removed.

Where the learner genuinely needs a concept to answer, the necessary cue is now placed **inside Q3B itself**. E210 and E212 are explicit examples of this repair.

Operating rule:

**Q3B = prompt-/stimulus-based free composition, not compulsory synthesis of Q1/Q2**

The learner may reuse P3 reasoning internally, but the visible task must stand on its own.

## 4. Final-review floor

B038–B039 state that full sets should protect at least five minutes of final review.

Two lessons violated that rule:
- E225: 4 minutes
- E231: 4 minutes

They now reserve 5 minutes:
- E225: Q1 38 / Q2 34 / Q3A 13 / Q3B 27 / Final review 5
- E231: Q1 35 / Q2 34 / Q3A 15 / Q3B 28 / Final review 5

A CI guard now checks E223–E234 and fails if the full-set review allocation falls below five minutes.

## 5. Q3A word-order quality

No new correction was needed.

The existing generic audit already checks:
- 8 chunks per item
- no oversized chunks
- designated 3rd / 5th positions cannot be answered by function words alone
- prompt order is not too close to answer order
- preserved answer-order adjacency is limited
- P4 contains at least 60 word-order items

This directly guards against the earlier failure mode where rearrangement items were only weakly shuffled.

## 6. Q3B / model-answer gradient

P4 contains 23 about-100-word writing prompts and 23 calibrated models.

The intended P4 difficulty gradient is **not**:
easy model → sophisticated model.

It is:
- explicit planning scaffold
- section integration
- fatigue
- harder stop-times
- reduced planning support
- fixed exam routine

Model answers therefore remain mostly within the same safe 90–110-word band while execution conditions become harder.

Late P4 should not reward ornamental complexity. It should reward reliable completion.

Rechecked on 2026-10-02.

The University of Tsukuba has published the 2027 selection outline, but the official application-guidelines page still lists the 2026 detailed general-selection guidelines. The detailed 2027 general-selection application guidelines are therefore still pending.
