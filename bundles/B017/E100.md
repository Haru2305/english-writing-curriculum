Integration Guide

mixed-source summaryでは、

Source 1 says...

Source 2 says...

と並べるだけでは弱い。

共通questionを先に置き、各資料をevidence / condition / limitationとして組み替える。



Reading

[1] A group of supermarkets is testing a program that reduces the price of prepared food near closing time. The purpose is to reduce edible food waste without encouraging customers to wait until the last hour for most purchases.



[2] Managers are watching two outcomes. The first is the share of prepared food left unsold at closing. The second is whether normal-price sales earlier in the day fall substantially. A discount may reduce waste but still be unattractive if it simply moves existing customers to a cheaper time of purchase.



[3] The stores are also experimenting with the timing of the discount. A very early reduction gives customers more time to notice the lower price, but it may affect more normal-price sales. A late reduction protects earlier revenue but may leave too little time to sell the remaining food. The pilot is therefore about finding a balance, not maximizing the number of discounted items sold.



[4] The first month suggests that the program can reduce unsold food, but the effect differs by store. Locations near railway stations sell many late items quickly, while some residential stores still have substantial waste after the discount begins. Managers plan to adjust timing by location rather than apply one fixed rule.



Data Table

Fictional first-month results



Store | Unsold share at closing | Earlier full-price sales | Discount start

A | 6% | Stable | 20:00

B | 9% | -3% | 19:30

C | 17% | Stable | 20:00



Notice

From next month, Store C will begin its discount 30 minutes earlier. Stores A and B will keep the current timing. Prices and product range will remain unchanged during the test. The company will compare unsold food and earlier full-price sales before and after the change.



Questions

Q1｜RQ13

Q2｜RQ09

## 解説の深掘り

### この問題で考えること
三つの資料を順番に紹介するのではなく、**「discount timingを変えると、wasteを減らしつつ earlier full-price sales を守れるか」**という一つの問いに統合する。

### 考え方
- Article：成功条件とtrade-offを定義する
- Table：現時点でどこに問題が残るかを示す
- Notice：次に何を変えて検証するかを示す

Store C はunsold shareが最も高い。一方、earlier full-price sales はstable。そこでdiscountを30分早めると、商品がdiscount priceで見つかる時間が延び、売れ残りを減らせる可能性がある。ただし早めすぎれば、通常価格で買っていた客が待つようになるかもしれない。これがmechanismを伴うtrade-off。

Noticeではpricesとproduct rangeを変えず、A/Bも現状維持なので、Cのtiming変更後の差を読みやすくしている。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「Store Cは17%なのでdiscountを増やすべき」  
○ 「Cでは現在のtimingでwasteが残るため開始を早め、その結果wasteが下がる一方でearlier salesが維持されるかを検証する」

前者は数値から直接solutionへ飛び、なぜtimingが効くのか、どんな副作用を見るべきかがない。

### 模範summaryの読み方
第1文でgoal＋trade-off、第2文でfirst-month evidence、第3文で次のcontrolled change＋何を学ぶtestか、をまとめている。source名を言わなくても、資料の**機能**が一つの論理に統合されている。

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
mixed-source summaryでは、**framework → evidence → next test**へ組み替える。店舗施策、交通pilot、学習支援、予約制度でも同じ処理が使える。
