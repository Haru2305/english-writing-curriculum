Article
A regional emergency network stores selected medicines at several hospitals so that treatment can continue when normal deliveries are disrupted. After one severe storm, the network reviewed its policy because one hospital had excess stock while another nearly exhausted a different medicine.
The review found that stock quantity was only part of the problem. Some medicines were used frequently and could rotate into normal care before expiration. Others were rarely needed but important during specific emergencies, making large inventories more likely to expire unused. Transport between hospitals also became slower when roads were blocked.
Rules
• Each hospital must hold a minimum emergency quantity for designated medicines.
• Stock above the minimum may be shared across hospitals during an emergency.
• Medicines approaching expiration should enter normal clinical use when appropriate and be replaced.
• Very rare medicines may be stored at fewer regional sites if rapid transport can normally be maintained.
• During transport disruption, hospitals must not assume immediate access to regional stock.

解答・解説・自己修正
Q1｜解答例
Because excessive stock can expire unused, needs differ by medicine, and regional sharing depends on transport conditions.
Q2｜解答例
Regional storage is useful only when medicines can reach hospitals quickly enough; disrupted transport can make distant stock unavailable when it is most needed.
Q3｜解答例
Medicine B, because it is highly important in emergencies but stored regionally and therefore vulnerable to transport delay.
Q4｜解答例
Frequently used medicines are easier to rotate into ordinary care before expiration, making large local stocks less wasteful than for rarely used medicines.

Model Japanese summary
緊急医薬品は量を増やせばよいのではなく、使用頻度、期限、輸送条件を合わせて設計する必要がある。日常使用の多い薬は更新しやすい一方、まれだが重要な薬は地域集約で廃棄を減らせる。ただし輸送途絶時には共有在庫へ依存できないため、各病院の最低備蓄も必要となる。
## 解説の考え方

### この問題で考えること
備蓄を「多いほど安全」とせず、**shortage risk / expiration risk / transport dependence** を同じ設計問題として扱う。

### 考え方
routine use が多い薬は在庫を通常診療へ回しやすく expiration risk を下げられる。rare but important な薬は地域集約で重複を減らせるが、transport disruption ではその便益が失われる。したがって **local minimum + regional sharing** は二者択一でなく、条件ごとに役割を分ける設計になる。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「重要な薬は全部の病院で大量保管」  
× 「まれな薬は全部地域集約」  
○ 使用頻度・期限・輸送途絶時のアクセスを比較し、最低備蓄と共有在庫を組み合わせる

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
備蓄・予備能力では、**平時の効率と障害時のaccess**を分ける。中央集約が効率的でも、移動経路が切れるとresilienceは下がる。


Self-correction
