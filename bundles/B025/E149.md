Service Note
A fictional city tested an AI chat service on its public-information website. The service answered routine questions about opening hours, required documents, application steps, and which department handled a request. It was not authorized to make eligibility decisions, interpret unusual personal circumstances, or change an official record.
During the trial, the service resolved many simple questions without staff intervention. However, several users continued chatting after receiving a general answer even though their situations contained exceptions. Staff concluded that the main design problem was not whether the system could produce fluent answers, but whether users could recognize when a general answer was no longer sufficient.
Operating Rules
• The chatbot may summarize information already published on official pages.
• It must not decide whether a user qualifies for a benefit.
• Questions involving conflicting records, urgent deadlines, or unusual exceptions must be transferred to staff.
• A transfer should preserve the conversation summary so users do not need to repeat the entire issue.
• The system must display the source page used for a factual answer whenever possible.

解答・解説・自己修正
Q1｜解答例
Opening-hours and routine document questions are the strongest candidates because they have high independent-resolution rates and relatively low correction rates.
Q2｜解答例
The main problem is user awareness: helping users recognize when a general answer no longer fits their case (the issue is neither fluency nor staff capacity), and transferring them to staff in time.
Q3｜解答例
The service improves access only when routine information is accurate and users with exceptions or record problems can be transferred reliably to human staff.
Q4｜解答例
Eligibility questions have a high correction rate, and Comment B shows that fluent wording can make a general answer sound more case-specific than it really is.

Model writing
The city should use the chatbot mainly for routine factual questions such as opening hours, standard document lists, and department directions. Questions about eligibility, conflicting records, or unusual exceptions should trigger transfer to staff. To reduce misunderstanding, every answer should show its official source and clearly state when it is only general guidance. The log data support this limited scope because routine questions have much lower correction rates than case-specific ones. However, the system still depends on current source pages and reliable transfer procedures, so staff oversight remains necessary. The city should also publish correction logs so recurring failure patterns remain visible.
## 解説の考え方

### この問題で考えること
AIが「賢いか」ではなく、**どのtaskなら誤りのコストを管理でき、どこから人間判断へ切り替えるべきか**を決める。

### 考え方
routine factual questions は high resolution / low correction なので自動化の候補になる。eligibility / record conflicts は case-specific judgment や official data が必要で correction rate も高い。ここから **task risk → scope → transfer trigger → misunderstanding safeguard** の順で制度を設計する。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「AIは便利だから導入」「誤答するから禁止」  
○ low-risk routine tasks に限定し、exception / eligibility / record conflict では人へ確実に移管する

### 模範解答の読み方
Model writing は Position（限定導入）→ task boundary → transfer trigger → source / general-guidance表示 → table根拠 → limitation（source freshness / transfer reliability）の順。human oversight は最後に付け足した注意ではなく、提案が成立する implementation condition である。

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
技術導入では **capability ではなく task boundary と failure cost** で自動化範囲を決める。
