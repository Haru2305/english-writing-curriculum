E173｜地域検診の予約枠：混合資料から受診障壁を減らす提案を書く

受診率の低さを「関心不足」と決めつけず、予約枠・時間帯・キャンセル・アクセスの資料から主要障壁を特定して改善案を書く。

Service Note
A community health program offers preventive screening appointments at three public clinics. The program sends invitations to eligible residents, but attendance differs sharply by location and time of day.
Staff initially assumed that people who did not attend were uninterested. Interviews suggested a more complicated picture. Some residents wanted screening but could not find appointments outside work hours. Others booked early, then cancelled late when plans changed. At one clinic, evening slots appeared fully booked for days even though several later went unused.
Rules
• Appointments may be booked up to 21 days in advance.
• Cancellation is allowed until the appointment start time.
• Same-day openings return to the booking system immediately.
• Each resident may hold one future screening appointment.
• Walk-in screening is not guaranteed.
Table
Clinic/time | Booking rate | No-show rate | Average cancellation before appointment | Public transport after 18:00
Central evening | 97% | 14% | 2 h | Frequent
North evening | 88% | 9% | 5 h | Limited
Central daytime | 72% | 5% | 16 h | Frequent
South daytime | 61% | 4% | 20 h | Moderate
User Comments
A: “I work until 17:30, so daytime appointments are difficult.”
B: “The evening slots look full, but sometimes I get a cancellation notice too late to rearrange my plans.”
C: “The North clinic has evening appointments, but the last bus home is early.”
Questions
Q1｜RQ13
Name two different barriers to attendance that are supported across the sources (for example, schedule availability, late release of slots, or transport).
Q2｜D03
Why would simply sending more invitations be an incomplete solution?
Q3｜RQ13
Which clinic/time period shows the clearest evidence of late-release inefficiency?
Proposal Writing
In 100–120 English words, recommend one priority change. Use at least two sources and explain one limitation or trade-off.
Self-check
□ I separated demand from access barriers.
□ I used cancellation timing and transport information.
□ I did not assume nonattendance means lack of interest.
□ I included one limitation or trade-off.

解答・解説・自己修正
Q1｜解答例
Any two of the following. Schedule availability: residents who work during the day cannot find usable appointments (Comment A). Late release: late cancellations return slots too late for others to use (Comment B, cancellation times in the table). Transport: some evening clinics have limited public transport after 18:00 (Comment C, table).
Q2｜解答例
Because awareness is not the only problem. Some residents already want screening but cannot find usable appointment times or cannot reach the clinic conveniently.
Q3｜解答例
Central evening, because booking is almost full, no-shows are relatively high, and cancellations occur only about two hours before the appointment.
Model writing
The program should require confirmation of popular evening appointments several hours before the visit. Central evening slots are almost fully booked, yet they also have the highest no-show rate and very late cancellations. User Comment B suggests that openings released at the last minute are difficult for other residents to use. Earlier confirmation could return unwanted slots to the system while there is still time to rearrange plans. However, this change would not solve transport barriers at the North clinic, so the program should evaluate booking efficiency separately from location access. That separation would show whether one intervention is solving the correct barrier.
## 解説の考え方

### この問題で考えること
nonattendance を「関心がない」と解釈せず、**wanting care と being able to use a slot** の間にあるbarrierを特定する。

### 考え方
Central evening はhigh booking + high no-show + very late cancellationなので、需要不足ではなく **usable slotがlate releaseで失われる**問題が強い。一方 North evening はtransport barrierが別にある。したがって一つのinterventionで全問題を解こうとせず、priority barrierを選び、その効果範囲を限定する。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「受診率が低い → 招待を増やす」  
○ 「希望者はいるが、slot timing / late release / transportが利用を妨げている。まず最も資料根拠が強いlate-release inefficiencyを処理する」

### 答案の組み立て
- **Position**: popular evening slotsにearly confirmation
- **Reason**: high-demand capacityがno-show / late cancellationで失われる
- **Mechanism**: earlier release → 他の希望者が予定を組み直せる
- **Support**: Central eveningの97% booking / 14% no-show / 2 h cancellation + Comment B
- **Qualification**: Northのtransport barrierは残る
- **Return**: booking efficiencyとlocation accessを別々に評価する

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
「利用しない人」をmotivationで説明する前に、**availability / timing / transport / transaction cost** のbarrierを確認する。
