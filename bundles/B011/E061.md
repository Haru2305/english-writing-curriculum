E061｜生成AIと仕事：研究結果はどこまで言い切れるか
P2 / LT02 / A08 筆者態度・確信度 / TF06

Stance Guide
strong claim: shows / demonstrates / clearly causes
moderate claim: suggests / is likely to / appears to
weak possibility: may / might / could
limitation: cannot establish / does not necessarily mean
P2では「何を言っているか」と「どこまで言っているか」を分ける。


Reading
[1] Reports about generative AI often use dramatic language. Some describe the technology as a direct cause of job loss, while others present it as a simple route to higher productivity. A careful analysis should avoid both shortcuts. When a new tool changes the time needed for a task, the result may affect staffing, wages, job design, or the amount of work an organization chooses to do. The direction of change is not fixed in advance.


[2] Imagine a study in which employees complete routine writing tasks more quickly when they use an AI assistant. Such a result would suggest that the tool can improve speed under the tested conditions. It would not by itself show that the same workers become more productive in every part of their jobs. Nor would it establish that firms will reduce employment. A company might use the saved time to increase output, redesign roles, or shift workers toward tasks that require judgment and interaction.


[3] The distinction matters because evidence can support claims of different strength. A controlled task may provide relatively strong evidence about performance on that task. It usually provides weaker evidence about long-term organizational change, where many other factors operate. Statements about employment therefore need more caution than statements about immediate task speed.


[4] The most useful conclusion is not that AI will certainly destroy jobs or certainly protect them. Current evidence may indicate changes in particular tasks and may help identify where larger effects are plausible. Stronger claims about occupations or labor markets require broader evidence collected across settings and over time.


[5] This approach also changes how readers should compare headlines. Two reports can describe the same result with very different levels of certainty. One may say that a tool “transforms work,” while another says that the evidence “suggests a possible change in task performance.” The second wording may sound less exciting, but it can be more faithful to the evidence. Careful reading therefore requires attention not only to the result being reported but also to how far the wording extends beyond what was directly measured.

Reading answers
Q1｜A
Paragraph 2〜3は、tested task上の速度改善には比較的直接的な根拠があるが、雇用全体への影響には距離があると区別している。


Q2｜B
筆者はAIの影響を否定していない。一方で、employmentについては“need more caution” “require broader evidence”と明示している。


Q3｜解答例
Task-level evidence directly measures performance under specific conditions, while labor-market outcomes depend on many additional factors. Therefore, broader employment claims require broader evidence.


A08｜筆者態度・確信度
主張の内容が同じ方向でも、強さは違う。
AI affects jobs.
AI may affect jobs.
The evidence suggests that AI affects some tasks.
The study does not establish a general employment effect.
この差を読むのがA08。


Short Output｜例
Available evidence suggests that AI may change some tasks, but it does not establish that AI necessarily causes job loss.
別解：AI may affect employment in some settings, but current evidence does not show that it necessarily causes job loss.
