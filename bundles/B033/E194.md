E194｜代理指標は目的そのものではない：高密度文から測定の限界を読む

P3 / LT03 / A25 / A29 / D05 / RQ04 / RQ09 / RF04 / SS01 / TF19

今日の狙い
初見概念 proxy measure を本文から構築し、測りやすい指標と本当に知りたい対象を区別して高密度文を分解する。

時間配分
Silent Plan 1分 / Reading 18分 / Dense Sentence 12分 / Questions 12分 / Translation 10分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 4分 ＝ Core 60分

Reading
[1] Many important qualities cannot be measured directly. A school may want to know whether students understand a subject, a company may want to know whether customers trust a service, and a researcher may want to know how healthy an ecosystem is. In each case, the target idea is broader than any single observable number.

[2] People therefore use proxy measures: observable quantities that stand in for something harder to measure. Test scores may serve as a proxy for understanding, repeat purchases as a proxy for trust, and the number of certain species as a proxy for ecosystem condition.

[3] A useful proxy is related to the target, but it is not identical to the target. A student can improve a score by learning the test format without gaining broader understanding. A customer may buy again because alternatives are unavailable rather than because trust is high.

[4] The problem becomes more serious when decisions strongly reward the proxy itself. Once people know that success will be judged by one number, they may change behavior to improve that number even when the underlying goal does not improve.

Dense Sentence
Although a proxy may initially correlate well with the condition it is intended to represent, using the proxy as the primary target of evaluation can alter the behavior that generated the correlation, thereby weakening the very relationship on which the proxy’s usefulness originally depended.

[5] This does not imply that proxy measures should be abandoned. Direct measurement may be impossible or too costly. The safer response is to use several indicators, examine how behavior changes under evaluation, and periodically check whether the proxy still tracks the broader goal.

Questions

Q3｜A29

Q4｜RQ09

Q5｜RQ09

解答・解説・自己修正

Q1｜解答例
A proxy measure is an observable quantity used to represent a broader condition that is difficult to measure directly.

Q2｜解答例
It concedes that direct measurement may be impossible or too expensive, so proxies cannot simply be discarded.

Q3｜解答例
A proxy may first be strongly related to the condition it represents. But if people are evaluated mainly by that proxy, they may change their behavior to improve the measured number. That behavioral change can weaken the original relationship between the proxy and the underlying condition.

Q4｜解答例
Because people may optimize the measured number rather than the underlying goal, changing the behavior that made the proxy informative in the first place.

Q5｜解答例
Multiple indicators reduce dependence on one imperfect measure and make it easier to notice when one proxy stops tracking the broader target.

Translation｜和訳例
代理指標は当初、それが表そうとする状態とよく相関していても、その代理指標自体を評価の主要目標にすると、もともとその相関を生み出していた行動が変化し、その結果、代理指標の有用性の根拠だった関係そのものが弱まることがある。

Self-correction
measure ≠ goal。proxyを改善した結果、targetとの関係が崩れるという因果を追う。
