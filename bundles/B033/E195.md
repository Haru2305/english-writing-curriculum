E195｜古い建物を残すとはどういうことか：二本文要約から本文連動作文へ

P3 / LT03 / D06 / RQ12 / RF05 / WF07 / WT03 / SS02 / TF06

今日の狙い
二つの保存方針を同じ軸で要約し、その要約内容を根拠として判断作文へつなげる。

時間配分
Silent Plan 1分 / Passage A 10分 / Passage B 10分 / Questions 9分 / English Summary 11分 / Summary-linked Writing 13分 / Self-check 2分 / Post-solve 4分 ＝ Core 60分

Passage A｜Preserve the Original Form
Supporters of strict preservation argue that historic buildings are valuable partly because their materials, layout, and visible traces of use carry evidence about the past. Replacing old walls, windows, or floor plans can make a building easier to use, but each alteration may remove information that cannot later be recovered.

This approach does not require freezing every building in time. Safety repairs may be necessary, and hidden systems such as wiring can sometimes be upgraded without changing the main historical features. The priority, however, is to minimize irreversible change.

Passage B｜Adaptive Reuse
Supporters of adaptive reuse begin from a different concern. A building that cannot serve a practical function may eventually be abandoned, poorly maintained, or demolished. Allowing new uses can provide the money and daily activity needed to keep the structure in operation.

Adaptive reuse may involve larger changes, such as adding elevators, changing interior partitions, or creating new entrances. The challenge is to distinguish features that carry essential historical meaning from features that can change without destroying the building’s identity.

Questions

Q2｜D06

Q3｜RQ09

Q4｜RQ09

解答・解説・自己修正

Q1｜解答例
Passage A prioritizes preservation of historical evidence and minimizing irreversible change. Passage B prioritizes keeping the building viable through continued practical use.

Q2｜解答例
Strict preservation favors reversible or minimal interventions but may restrict new functions. Adaptive reuse accepts larger physical changes when they support continued use, provided essential historical features are protected.

Q3｜解答例
If a building cannot serve a practical function, it may lose funding and daily use, increasing the risk of neglect or demolition.

Q4｜解答例
It must decide which features are essential to the building’s historical identity and which can be changed without destroying that identity.

Model summary
Strict preservation seeks to protect historic evidence by minimizing irreversible changes to materials and layout, although necessary repairs are allowed. Adaptive reuse gives greater weight to continued practical use, arguing that an unused building may eventually be neglected or demolished. It therefore accepts larger changes when they help keep the structure viable. The central trade-off is between preserving original features and allowing modifications that support long-term use, while both approaches require judgments about which changes are acceptable.

Model writing
I would allow adaptive reuse of the old station while protecting a defined set of historically important features. The passages show that an unused building can become vulnerable to neglect, so a new function may help finance maintenance and keep the site active. At the same time, irreversible changes can destroy historical evidence. The city should therefore identify essential exterior and interior features before approving alterations and require new additions to be as reversible as practical. This policy uses continued use to support preservation rather than treating the two goals as complete opposites.

## 解説の使い方

### この問題で考えること
「保存か活用か」の二択ではない。**不可逆な歴史的損失を抑えながら、建物を使い続けられる条件をどう作るか**が判断の核心。

### 考え方
Passage A は **irreversible change を減らす**、Passage B は **continued use が維持資金と日常的管理を支える**という別の基準を置く。  
作文では、要約で得た二軸をそのまま使い、  
**未利用 → 維持資金・活動が減る → neglect / demolition risk が上がる**  
と  
**大きな改変 → historical evidence が失われる**  
を両方残す。そこで「重要部分を先に定義し、変更はできるだけ可逆的にする」という条件が両者をつなぐ。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「古い建物は大切だから残すべき」  
× 「使われていないから新しくすべき」  
○ 「使われないこと自体が保存を危うくする一方、不可逆な改変も価値を失わせるので、continued use と reversibility を同時に設計する」  
一方の価値を言うだけでなく、**trade-off を条件付きの方針へ変えている**。

### 模範解答の読み方
- Position：adaptive reuse を認めつつ重要部分を保護
- Reason：unused building は neglect に弱い
- Mechanism：new function が maintenance と daily activity を支える
- Qualification：irreversible change は historical evidence を失わせる
- Safeguard：essential features の事前特定＋可能な限り reversible
- Return：利用と保存を対立させず、利用を保存の手段にする

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
二つの価値が衝突する問題では、**AかBか**ではなく、  
**「Aの便益を残しながら、Bの不可逆な損失をどの条件で抑えるか」**  
まで考える。

B：要約と作文はそれぞれ成立するが、両者の接続が弱い。
