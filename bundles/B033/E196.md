E196｜再現実験の結果が一致しないとき：手順書・実験メモ・表を統合する

P3 / LT03 / D04 / D10 / RQ13 / RF06 / SS05 / TF19

今日の狙い
手順・実施条件・結果表を統合し、結果の不一致を単純な成功/失敗ではなく条件差として日本語で説明する。

時間配分
Silent Plan 1分 / Protocol 8分 / Lab Note 8分 / Table 8分 / Questions 11分 / Integration Plan 5分 / Japanese Summary 14分 / Self-check 2分 / Post-solve 4分 ＝ Core 60分

Protocol
A fictional experiment tests whether a dye fades faster under stronger light. The protocol specifies identical paper, equal dye volume, a constant distance from the lamp, and a fixed exposure time of four hours. Samples are photographed before and after exposure, and color loss is calculated from the images.

Lab Note
Team A followed the protocol in a room kept near 22°C. Team B used the same lamp model and exposure time, but its room warmed to 31°C during the experiment. Team B also placed a small fan near the samples after noticing the heat. Neither temperature nor airflow had been specified in the original protocol.

Results Table
Team | Low light color loss | High light color loss | Room condition
Original | 8% | 24% | 22–23°C, still air
A | 9% | 23% | 21–22°C, still air
B | 15% | 29% | 28–31°C, fan used

[1] Team A closely reproduced the original pattern. Team B also found greater fading under stronger light, but both of its values were higher.

[2] The difference does not show that Team B “failed” to reproduce the experiment in every sense. The direction of the light effect remained similar. However, because temperature and airflow differed, Team B’s absolute values cannot be compared as if only light intensity changed.

[3] A useful follow-up would specify temperature and airflow, then repeat the comparison. The discrepancy therefore reveals not only variation in results but also an ambiguity in the original protocol.

Questions
Q1｜RQ13
Which sources identify the uncontrolled conditions in Team B?

Q2｜D04
What causal explanation is plausible for Team B’s higher color loss?

Q3｜D10
How does paragraph 2 qualify the statement that Team B failed to reproduce the experiment?

Q4｜RQ09
Why does the discrepancy reveal a weakness in the original protocol?

Japanese Summary

Self-check

---

解答・解説・自己修正

Q1｜解答例
The Lab Note identifies high temperature and fan use, while the Results Table confirms that Team B’s room conditions differed from the original and Team A.

Q2｜解答例
Higher temperature or airflow may have increased drying or fading, but the data do not isolate which condition caused the difference.

Q3｜解答例
Team B reproduced the direction of the light effect—more fading under stronger light—even though its absolute values differed, so reproduction was partial rather than simply absent.

Q4｜解答例
Because temperature and airflow were not specified, two teams could follow the written protocol yet operate under materially different conditions.

Model Japanese summary
