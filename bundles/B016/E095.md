E095｜地域防災情報：掲示とFAQから改善策を選ぶ
P2 / LT03 / SS04 / WF04 Guided / WF05 Guided / TF17
今日の狙い
非連続資料からproblemを特定し、限られた時間内に改善策の優先順位とmechanismを書く。情報探索と英作文を別々にしすぎず、必要情報だけを拾って90〜110語へつなぐ。
時間配分
Timed Source Guide 4分 / Notice・FAQ 12分 / Questions 8分 / Writing Task 20分 / Revision 7分 / Self-check 4分 / Post-solve 5分 ＝ Core 60分
Timed Source Guide
最初の4分で、
- 誰が困っているか
- どの条件が障壁か
- どのsolutionが直接作用するか
だけを抜く。資料の全文暗記は不要。
Notice
Emergency Information Registration
Residents can receive evacuation updates by smartphone app or email.
App users receive location-specific alerts automatically.
Email users must select their district when registering.
Paper notices are posted at community centers after an alert is issued.
Registration support is available on weekdays from 9:00 to 17:00.
FAQ
Q: Do I need a smartphone?
A: No. Email alerts are available, and paper notices are also posted.
Q: I do not use the internet. How will I know about an alert at night?
A: Paper notices may not provide immediate nighttime information. Residents are encouraged to arrange another alert method with family, neighbors, or local support services.
Q: Why are some residents not registered?
A: A recent local survey found that some residents were unaware of the service, while others knew about it but found weekday registration support difficult to use.
Q: What change is being considered?
A: The city is considering one monthly evening registration session and a simple printed guide explaining the available alert methods.
Questions
Q1｜RQ09
What can reasonably be inferred about the limits of paper notices?
Q2｜D03
Which proposed change most directly addresses the barrier faced by residents who already know about the service but cannot use weekday support?
Writing Task
Write 90–110 English words recommending which improvement should be tested first. Include:
- the barrier you prioritize
- why your chosen action addresses it
- one reason the other action may still matter
- one limitation of your recommendation
- one measure of success
Self-check

□ I explained why the solution should affect that barrier.
□ I acknowledged value in the alternative.

解答・解説・自己修正
Reading answers
Q1｜解答例
Paper notices may be too slow for immediate nighttime communication, so they cannot by themselves provide timely alerts in every situation.
別解：Notice によれば掲示は警報の発令後に行われるため、即時性や届く範囲に限界がある。
Q2
The monthly evening registration session.
WF04 Guided
evening support → residents blocked by weekday hours
というproblem-solution対応を明示する。
WF05 Guided
printed guideはawareness/understanding、evening sessionはtime-access barrierへ作用する。何を優先するかはcriterion次第。
Model answer
The city should first test a monthly evening registration session because it directly addresses residents who know about the alert service but cannot use weekday support. A printed guide could still help people understand the different alert methods, so it should not be abandoned. However, one evening session may reach only a small number of residents. The city should run the trial for three months and record attendance, completed registrations, and whether participants had previously been unable to use daytime support. That evidence would show whether access hours are a significant barrier.
Self-correction
資料中の「知らない人」と「知っているが登録支援時間が合わない人」を混同していないか確認する。
到達目安
A：SS04から障壁を特定し、優先順位付きsolutionを時間内に書ける。
B：資料理解は正しいが、alternativeとの比較が弱い。
C：最初に目立つsolutionを選び、誰のproblemかを書かない。
