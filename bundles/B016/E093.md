E093｜学習支援予算：複数基準で優先順位を決める
P2 / LT01 / WF05 評価・優先順位 / SS03 / TF05
今日の狙い
複数の候補を好みで選ばず、本文が定めるcriteriaを表へ適用して優先順位を説明する。90〜110語の英作文でcriteria→evaluation→choiceを一貫させる。
時間配分
Priority Guide 6分 / Reading 13分 / Option Table 7分 / Questions 8分 / Writing Task 18分 / Self-check 4分 / Post-solve 4分 ＝ Core 60分
Priority Guide
優先順位では、
1. criteriaを先に置く
2. 全候補を同じ軸で見る
3. trade-offを明示する
4. “I like...”ではなくcriteriaに戻ってchoiceを出す
Reading
[1] A school has a limited fund for one new learning-support project. The school’s stated goal is not simply to serve the largest number of students. It wants to reduce a barrier that currently prevents students from using existing learning opportunities.
[2] The committee agrees on three criteria. First, the project should address a documented barrier rather than add a convenience already available to most students. Second, it should be usable by a broad range of students without requiring expensive personal equipment. Third, the school should be able to evaluate the project within one term.
[3] Cost still matters, but the cheapest option is not automatically the best. A slightly more expensive project may deserve priority if it addresses a stronger barrier and can be tested clearly. Conversely, a popular idea may rank lower if it mainly improves convenience for students who already have good access.

Q1｜RQ13

Q2｜RQ09

Writing Task
Write 90–110 English words recommending one project. Your answer must:
- state two criteria you consider most important
- compare your chosen option with at least one alternative
- acknowledge one trade-off
- make a clear recommendation
Self-check
□ I used the same criteria across options.
□ My recommendation follows from the criteria.
□ I included a trade-off rather than hiding a weakness.
□ I did not choose only by popularity or cost.

解答・解説・自己修正
Reading answers
Q1
Option B. It addresses a documented scheduling barrier, does not require personal equipment, and can be evaluated through a one-term pilot.
Q2｜解答例
Because the school’s goal is to reduce an access barrier, and expected user numbers do not show whether an option reaches students who are currently excluded or whether the effect can be evaluated.
WF05｜評価・優先順位
criteria → evaluation → choice
の順を崩さない。
「Bが良いからcriteriaもBに合うように選ぶ」のではなく、criteriaを先に固定して候補を比べる。
Model answer
The school should prioritize Option B because it directly addresses a documented scheduling barrier and can be tested within one term without requiring students to own special equipment. Option A may attract more users, but it mainly adds convenience to a service that many students can already access. Option B is slightly more expensive than the cheapest choice, yet the stronger connection to the school’s access goal justifies the extra cost. A one-term pilot would also provide clear evidence for the next funding decision. That makes the pilot both targeted and easy to evaluate.
Self-correction
recommendationを隠してcriteriaだけ書いていないか、逆にchoiceだけで比較軸がないか確認する。
到達目安
A：複数基準で候補を比較し、trade-off込みの優先順位を説明できる。
B：criteriaはあるが、候補比較が一部だけ。
C：好み・人気・最大値だけでchoiceを決める。
