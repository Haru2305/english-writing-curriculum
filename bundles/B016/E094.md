E094｜学校図書館の改善：解決策と優先順位を一つの答案で示す
P2 / LT03 / WF04 Guided / WF05 Guided / WQ04 Transfer / TF05
今日の狙い
複数の改善案から優先順位を決め、その選択がproblemへどう作用するかまで一つの段落で説明する。WQ04を別文脈Free answerとして確実に回収し、criteria→choice→mechanism→limitationを100〜120語で通す。
時間配分
Decision Guide 5分 / Reading 14分 / Questions 7分 / Writing Task 22分 / Revision 6分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 3分 ＝ Core 60分
Decision Guide
1. problemを一つに絞る
2. criteriaを2つ決める
3. priorityを決める
4. mechanismを書く
5. limitationと次の確認方法を書く
Reading
[1] A school library is crowded after classes, but the pattern is uneven. Many students use tables for group work, while students who want silent study often leave when no quiet seats remain. The school has a small improvement budget and cannot enlarge the room this year.
[2] Three ideas are being discussed: adding more shared tables, creating a quiet zone with movable partitions, or extending opening hours by one hour. More tables could serve popular group work, but they would not directly address the shortage of quiet space. Longer hours could spread demand over time, although many students depend on buses that leave soon after school.
[3] A quiet zone would reduce flexible floor space, but it directly targets the conflict reported by students who leave because of noise. The school could test the arrangement for four weeks and record both use of the quiet zone and whether group-work congestion becomes worse elsewhere.
[4] The best first step depends on the goal. If the goal is to maximize total seats, more tables may rank highly. If the goal is to reduce a documented access barrier for silent study, the quiet-zone pilot has a stronger connection to the problem.
Questions
Q1｜D03
Which proposal most directly addresses the documented silent-study barrier?
Q2｜WF05
Why might “most popular use” and “highest priority” lead to different choices?
Writing Task
Write 100–120 English words recommending the school’s first action. Include:
- two criteria
- comparison with at least one alternative
- how your proposal addresses the problem
- one limitation
- one way to evaluate the trial
Self-check
□ My criteria appear before or clearly support my choice.
□ I explained the mechanism, not only the proposal.
□ I compared at least one alternative fairly.
□ I included a real limitation.
□ My paragraph is easy to follow without extra explanation.

解答・解説・自己修正
Reading answers
Q1
The quiet-zone pilot.
Q2｜解答例
The most popular activity may already be well served, while priority can instead be based on reducing a barrier faced by students who currently lose access.
WF04 Guided
提案がproblemのどの原因へ作用するかを明示する。
WF05 Guided
同じcriteriaを複数案に当て、choiceを後から出す。
WQ04 Transfer
E087導入から別文脈でFree answer。claim / criteria / mechanism / limitation / evaluation の順で、読み手が判断過程を追えるかを見る。
Model answer
The school should first test a quiet zone because the strongest criteria are reducing a documented access barrier and producing clear evidence within a short period. Adding more group tables might serve many students, but it would not help those who currently leave because they cannot study quietly. Movable partitions could create a quieter area without permanent construction. The main limitation is that group-work space would become smaller, so the school should monitor congestion as well as quiet-zone use during a four-week pilot before making the change permanent. This would let the school compare academic benefit with lost collaborative space.
別解：goalを「総座席数の最大化」とするなら more tables を選んでもよい。ただし基準を明示し、その案の限界と評価方法を述べること。
Self-correction
choiceを先に決めてcriteriaを後付けしていないか確認する。alternativeの弱点だけを書き、自案の弱点を隠していれば修正する。
