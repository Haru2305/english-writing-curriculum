# B037 — Tsukuba P4 Days 13–18

## Purpose

Complete the semi-full phase. Day 12's 120-minute B036 Checkpoint is the first full-format diagnostic exposure; it is not treated as a new minimum daily workload. Days 13–14 intentionally remove one late section at a time so the learner can repair fatigue-specific Q3A/Q3B weakness identified by Day 12. From Day 15 onward, repeated 120-minute four-section runs become the default.

B037 therefore moves from targeted post-diagnostic reinforcement to stable full-set execution, with emphasis on preserving Q3A/Q3B accuracy after two reading sections.

## Sequence

- E217 / Day 13 / 100 min: two readings + Q3A; protect designated-position accuracy after reading fatigue
- E218 / Day 14 / 110 min: two readings + Q3B; protect about-100-word composition after reading fatigue
- E219 / Day 15 / 120 min: first stabilized four-section run
- E220 / Day 16 / 120 min: heavier front half; maintain Q3A/Q3B quality under fatigue
- E221 / Day 17 / 120 min: overrun-recovery drill with absolute stop times
- E222 / Day 18 / 120 min: B037 semi-full final checkpoint and gate into B038 full sets

Design: TPL-v4-P4-TSK.

Priority:
1. strict character-limit Japanese explanation across two readings
2. hard section stop-times
3. designated-position accuracy after fatigue
4. about-100-word composition with concrete example/safeguard
5. preserve final review time
6. recover from local delay without abandoning later sections
