Reading
Public consultation does not mean that every request must be accepted. A city may receive dozens of competing suggestions about a transport plan, housing project, or environmental rule. Some requests may conflict with one another, exceed the available budget, or undermine the original purpose of the policy.


Officials may therefore reject a proposal after considering it. The important question is why. A proposal can be rejected because it is too costly, legally impossible, poorly supported by evidence, or inconsistent with an agreed objective. Explaining the reason allows people to evaluate the decision even when they disagree with it.


Refusal describes a different action. A resident may refuse to answer an optional survey question, or a company may refuse to provide information that it is not legally required to disclose. The focus is on declining to perform an action rather than evaluating a proposal.


Decline is often more formal or polite. An organization may decline an invitation to join a working group because it lacks staff. An official may decline to comment on a confidential negotiation. The word does not necessarily imply conflict.


Dismiss is more dangerous in public decision making. Officials may ultimately reject a request, but they should not dismiss a concern merely because it is inconvenient. A concern about accessibility, safety, or unequal impact may reveal a real problem even when the proposed solution is not adopted.


Good consultation therefore separates hearing from agreeing. Decision makers can reject particular proposals, decline some requests, or refuse actions that are inappropriate while still acknowledging legitimate concerns. Accountability depends on showing that objections were considered rather than simply dismissed.

Language Focus
1. A reject
proposalを評価して退けます。


2. A refuse
refuse to answer の型です。


3. A decline
invitationをformalに断ります。


4. A dismiss
concernを軽視して退ける意味です。


Why these words?
「反対する・断る」を一語で処理せず、何を退けるのかを見ます。
proposalなのか、actionなのか、formal offerなのか、concernの重要性なのかで選びます。

解説補強｜proposal rejectionとconcern dismissalを分ける

### この問題で考えること
意見を採用しないことと、意見の背景にある問題を無視することは別です。

### 考え方
resident raises concern
→ proposed remedyをevaluate
→ remedy自体はrejectしてもよい
→ underlying concernはmeasure / explain / alternative responseで扱う
→ reasonを公開してaccountabilityを保つ

### 弱い考え方との比較
× The city rejected the request, so the concern was unimportant.
○ A specific request can be rejected while the underlying concern remains relevant to the final decision.

### 模範解答の読み方
rejectはproposal、refuseはaction、declineはformal offer/request、dismissはconcernのimportanceを下げる語です。objectの種類がdecision process上の役割を示します。
