E131｜避難支援の登録：記事・FAQ・表から原因を日本語で統合する

P3 / LT03 / D07 Transfer / RF06 / RQ13 / SS05 / TF17

混合資料全体が答えようとしている問いを特定し、記事・立場資料・FAQ・表の情報を一つの日本語要約へ統合する。資料ごとの列挙で終わらせない。

Source A

A fictional city offers transport assistance for residents who may have difficulty reaching an emergency shelter. After a community drill, officials noticed a puzzling result: many people who appeared eligible for assistance had not registered for it. The central question was not whether the service existed, but why intended users were failing to enter the system.



Interviews suggested several answers. Some residents had seen general disaster-preparation information but did not realize that transport assistance required advance registration. Others assumed the service was only for people with severe physical disabilities, even though the program also covered some residents with temporary mobility problems or no access to a vehicle. A smaller group knew about the program but found the application instructions difficult to locate.



Officials therefore changed the information flow. Instead of placing transport details on a separate webpage, they added a short explanation to the main shelter guide and repeated it in neighborhood notices. They also clarified that registration did not guarantee the same form of transport in every emergency; the city would choose an available method based on conditions.



Source B

Community coordinator:

“People often hear the phrase ‘transport assistance’ but do not know what action they are expected to take. Information is useful only when residents can connect it to a concrete step.”



Emergency planner:

“We also need to avoid promising a specific vehicle or route in advance. The purpose of registration is to identify needs early enough to plan, not to guarantee one fixed response.”



FAQ

Q: Who should consider registering?

A: Residents who may be unable to reach a designated shelter without transport support should review the eligibility conditions.



Q: Does registration guarantee a private vehicle?

A: No. Registration identifies a need for assistance, but the method depends on available resources and the emergency situation.



Q: When should residents register?

A: Before an emergency whenever possible, because requests made after an evacuation begins may be harder to coordinate.

Reading answers

Q1｜解答例

Why were residents who might need transport assistance failing to register even though the service already existed?



Q2｜解答例

Groups A and B both knew the service existed, but registration was much higher when residents also knew that advance registration was required.



Q3｜解答例

The coordinator emphasizes turning general awareness into a concrete action, while the planner emphasizes setting realistic expectations about what registration guarantees.



Q4｜解答例

Separate examples of misunderstanding can be compressed into lack of procedural clarity; repeated notices and FAQ changes can be compressed into improved information flow.

Model summary

避難時の輸送支援は存在していても、対象者が事前登録の必要性や対象条件を理解せず利用につながっていなかった。表でも、制度を知るだけの群より手続きまで理解した群で登録率が高い。市は情報を申請行動につなげつつ、登録が特定手段を保証しない点も明確にする必要がある。



Self-correction

資料名を主語にせず、「何が問題で、どの根拠から、どう改善すべきか」を一つの説明として書く。
