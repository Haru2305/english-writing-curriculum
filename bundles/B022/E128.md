E128｜地域博物館の無料開館日：複数段落を日本語で要約する

P3 / LT01 / RF06 日本語要約 / RQ11 / TF15



今日の狙い

英語本文を毎文訳さずに理解し、重要情報と論理関係だけを日本語へ圧縮する。具体例を落としても、目的・結果・限界・示唆を残す。



時間配分

Japanese Summary Guide 6分 / Reading 18分 / Questions 10分 / Summary Plan 8分 / Summary Task 10分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 5分 ＝ Core 60分



Japanese Summary Guide

1. 目的を一文で取る

2. 結果と限界を分ける

3. 具体例は、結論に必要なものだけ残す

4. 日本語として自然でも、本文の因果や限定を変えない



Reading

[1] A regional museum tested one free-admission day each month for six months. The goal was not simply to increase total attendance. The museum wanted to learn whether price had been preventing people who rarely visited cultural institutions from entering at all. Visitors were therefore asked whether they had come before, how far they had traveled, and what had influenced their decision.



[2] Attendance on the free days was substantially higher than on ordinary weekdays. More importantly, first-time visitors made up a larger share of the audience. Families with school-age children and younger adults were especially likely to say that free admission had encouraged them to try the museum. These results suggest that price was a real barrier for some groups.



[3] The trial also revealed limits. Many visitors who lived far from the museum said that transport time mattered more than the ticket price. Others explained that they could visit only on weekends, while the trial had been held on a weekday. A free ticket therefore did not remove every obstacle. The museum also found that some people who attended once on a free day did not return during the following months.



[4] This last point matters because a crowded free day can make a program look more successful than it is. If success is defined only as the number of entries on the trial day, the policy appears clearly effective. If the aim is to broaden long-term participation, however, staff also need to know who returned, which groups remained absent, and whether visitors were able to use the museum comfortably when attendance was high.



[5] Staff initially considered expanding free admission to every weekday. However, the survey suggested that a more targeted approach might be more useful. A weekend free day could reach people who were constrained by work or school schedules, while discounted family programs could lower costs without eliminating ticket revenue on all days. Better information about bus routes might also help visitors who saw travel as the main difficulty.



[6] The trial did not show that admission fees were unimportant, nor did it prove that free entry alone would create lasting participation. It showed that reducing price can attract new visitors, but access depends on several barriers at once. The museum’s next step should therefore be based on which groups it wants to reach and which obstacles those groups actually face.



Questions

Summary Task

本文を120〜150字の日本語で要約する。

必ず「調査目的」「主要結果」「限界」「次の判断への示唆」を含める。



Self-check

□ 英文を文ごとに訳して並べていない。

□ 目的と結果を区別した。

□ 無料化の効果を過大評価していない。

□ 具体例より上位の結論を優先した。

解答・解説・自己修正



Reading answers

Q1｜解答例

Free admission attracted more visitors, including first-time visitors, but lasting access depended on other barriers such as schedule, transport, and repeat participation.



Q2｜解答例

The purpose of the trial, the increase in attendance/first-time visitors, the remaining barriers, and the implication that future measures should target specific obstacles.



Q3｜解答例

Because price was only one barrier, and the trial did not show that free entry created sustained participation or removed transport and schedule constraints.



RF06｜日本語要約

英語を日本語へ置換する課題ではなく、本文の情報階層を保って圧縮する課題。

purpose → result → limitation → implication

の骨格を先に作り、その後で自然な日本語へ整える。



Model summary

地域博物館は、料金が来館の障壁かを確かめるため、月一度の無料開館日を半年試した。来館者と初来館者は増えたが、交通や曜日、再来館といった課題が残り、無料化だけで継続的な利用が生まれるとは言えなかった。今後は、呼びたい層を決め、その層が直面する障壁に応じた施策を選ぶ必要がある。



Self-correction

「無料にしたら人が増えた」で止めず、何を調べ、何が分かり、何がまだ分からないかを残す。
