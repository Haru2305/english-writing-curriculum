Reading
Machine translation has changed how quickly people can understand text in another language. A traveler can translate a sign in seconds, a student can get a rough idea of an unfamiliar passage, and an organization can process large volumes of routine material at relatively low cost. These uses show why automatic translation can expand access to information.


Speed, however, is not the same as accuracy in every context. A sentence may be grammatically simple but still depend on cultural knowledge, tone, or information from earlier in a conversation. A word with several meanings can be translated correctly only if the system identifies which meaning fits the situation.


For low-risk tasks, an imperfect translation may still be useful. If a person wants to understand the general topic of a restaurant review, a small error may not matter. The same error can be more serious in medical instructions, legal documents, safety warnings, or confidential business communication.


This difference changes the role of human translators. Automatic tools can complement human work by producing a first draft, identifying repeated phrases, or handling routine sections. A human can then focus attention on ambiguity, tone, unusual terminology, and consequences that require judgment.


Human review can also compensate for weaknesses that are hard to detect automatically. A system may produce a fluent sentence that sounds natural while changing an important detail. Fluency alone therefore does not prove that the meaning is correct. Review is especially important when a translation contains names, measurements, instructions, or terms whose small differences can change a decision. In those cases, the reviewer is not simply correcting awkward language; the reviewer is checking whether the translated text preserves the function and consequences of the original.


Specialized resources can supplement the technology. A controlled glossary may help keep terminology consistent, and examples from previous translations can provide context for repeated expressions. These resources do not remove the need for judgment, but they can make both human and automated work more reliable. Organizations also need procedures for privacy and confidentiality. Sending sensitive text to an inappropriate system may create a risk even if the translation itself is accurate. The quality of a translation process therefore depends on both linguistic performance and the way the tool is integrated into a wider workflow.


The appropriate balance depends on purpose and risk. Requiring full human review for every informal message may waste time, while relying only on automation for a high-stakes document may create unnecessary danger. A sensible system uses automation where speed matters and adds human judgment where errors carry greater costs.


The useful question is therefore not whether machines or humans are better in general. It is which tasks can be automated safely, which tasks benefit from human interpretation, and how the strengths of each can complement the other.

Writing
モデル答案
Machine translation can complement human translators when large amounts of routine text must be processed quickly. A specialized glossary can supplement the system with consistent terminology, while human review can compensate for errors involving context or tone. Full review is especially important when a translation affects health, safety, or legal decisions.

解説補強｜riskでhuman reviewの深さを変える

### この問題で考えること
machine translationのaccuracyを一律に判断せず、**同じerrorでも結果の重大さが違う**ことを判断基準にします。

### 考え方
routine / low-stakes text → speed benefitが大きく、light reviewでもよい場合がある
high-stakes text → context errorがhealth / legal decisionへ伝わる
→ human reviewの価値が上がる

### 弱い考え方との比較
× Machine translation is fast, so it should replace translators.
○ Automation can handle routine text, while human review compensates for contextual weaknesses where errors have serious consequences.

### 模範解答の読み方
complement は役割分担、supplement は追加資源、compensate for は弱点の補填です。3語の意味差が、そのまま**system designの違い**になっています。

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
technology支援では **task type × error consequence × review level** を決めます。
