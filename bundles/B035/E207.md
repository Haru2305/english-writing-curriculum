E207｜筑波Day3：大問3A―整序3問で「3番目・5番目」を正確に答える

P4 / Tsukuba Day 3 / Q3A-style word order / Core 60

今日の狙い
近年の筑波大問3Aと同様に、文脈中の整序を完成させたうえで、指定された3番目・5番目の語句を正確に答える。

時間配分
Context reading 10分 / Ordering 25分 / Syntax audit 12分 / Position check 8分 / Self-check 2分 / Post-solve 3分 ＝ Core 60分

Context

A university study examined how people judge online reviews. Participants were shown comments written by previous customers. Some comments included detailed reasons, while others contained only a numerical rating. Researchers found that readers were more likely to remember a criticism when it included a concrete example, even when the numerical rating was identical.

Ordering

1. What made the detailed comments useful
(why the reviewer / that / rather than merely giving / readers / was / a rating / they showed / was dissatisfied).

2. How useful a rating is
(whether readers understand / less on / it / than on / depends / represents / the number itself / what).

3. Examples
(that would otherwise / more accurately / abstract / a criticism / were remembered / to readers / when they illustrated / have remained).

解答方法
各問について、完成した英文の
- 3番目
- 5番目
の語句だけを答えなさい。

Syntax Audit

A. Q1の形式目的語 it は何を指すか。
B. Q2で “less useful than it seems at first” の比較構造を説明せよ。
C. Q3の “to have been remembered” はなぜ完了受動不定詞になるか。
D. 各問で、まず確定すべき「主語＋述語」を書け。

Self-check
□ 全文を完成してから3番目・5番目を数えた
□ 句単位で数え間違えていない
□ 完了・受動・比較を構文根拠で処理した

---

解答・解説・自己修正

## 解答一覧
1｜3番目 the number itself / 5番目 why the reviewer
2｜3番目 when they illustrated / 5番目 whether readers understand
3｜3番目 when they illustrated / 5番目 that would otherwise


1 完成
What made the detailed comments useful was that they showed readers why the reviewer was dissatisfied rather than merely giving a rating.
3番目: they showed
5番目: why the reviewer

2 完成
How useful a rating is depends less on the number itself than on whether readers understand what it represents.
3番目: the number itself
5番目: whether readers understand

3 完成
Examples were remembered more accurately when they illustrated a criticism that would otherwise have remained abstract to readers.
3番目: when they illustrated
5番目: that would otherwise

Syntax Audit
A. 後続の to understand ... の内容を受ける形式目的語。
B. less useful が比較級、than 以下が比較対象。
C. remember されたことが found より前に成立しているため。
D. 1 Detailed comments made / 2 A rating ... may be / 3 Researchers found

自己修正
本番では「全文は合っていたのに3・5番目を数え違えた」をゼロにする。完成→番号付け→転記の順を固定。

到達目安
A：3問すべて構文完成＋位置正答
B：全文は作れるが位置ミス1つ
C：構文自体が不安定
