E208｜筑波Day4：大問3B―英文資料を踏まえて80〜100語で意見を書く

P4 / Tsukuba Day 4 / Q3B-style source-linked writing / Core 60

今日の狙い
資料条件を取りこぼさず、80〜100語で立場・理由・具体策・限定を安全な英文でまとめる。

時間配分
Source reading 10分 / Issue extraction 8分 / Plan 7分 / Draft 15分 / Revision 12分 / Rewrite 5分 / Self-check 1分 / Post-solve 2分 ＝ Core 60分

Source

A university is considering replacing some printed course handouts with a digital-only system.

Supporters argue that digital distribution reduces printing costs, allows instructors to correct errors quickly, and makes materials searchable. Students can also access the same files from several locations.

Critics point out that not all students prefer long periods of screen reading. Some students annotate more effectively on paper, and device or network problems can interrupt access. Printing materials individually may also shift costs from the university to students.

The university does not need to choose between printing everything and printing nothing. One proposal is to provide digital files by default while allowing students to request printed versions for long readings or accessibility needs.

Task
Write 80–100 English words answering the following question:

Should the university adopt the proposed “digital by default, print on request” system?

Your answer must:
- clearly state your position
- use at least two ideas from the source
- include one limitation or condition
- remain within 80–100 words

解答・解説・自己修正

Model answer (92 words)
I support a digital-by-default system if printed copies remain easy to request. Digital files can reduce university printing costs and allow instructors to correct mistakes quickly, while students can search the material on their devices. However, digital access should not become a barrier for students who read long texts more effectively on paper or who have unreliable devices. The university should therefore provide printed versions without complicated procedures for long readings and accessibility needs. This approach keeps most of the efficiency of digital distribution while preserving a practical alternative.

構成
1. Position
2. Benefit 1
3. Benefit 2
4. Limitation
5. Conditional recommendation

減点回避
- “I agree”だけで始めず何に賛成か明示
- sophisticatedな語彙より正確な基本文
- source wordingの長いコピーを避ける
- 80〜100語から外れない
