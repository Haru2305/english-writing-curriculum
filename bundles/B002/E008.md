Reading
Personal data can make digital services more convenient. A navigation app can remember common routes, a health service can adjust reminders to a user's needs, and an online store can make recommendations based on previous choices. These functions may reduce effort and make services feel more relevant.


The same data can also reveal sensitive information. A record created for one purpose may later be useful for another, and users may not always understand how long data will be stored or who can access it. For this reason, some people argue for stricter limits on secondary use. Others argue against rules that are so restrictive that useful services become difficult to provide.


The disagreement is not simply between people who value privacy and people who value convenience. Many users want both. The difficult questions concern how much data is necessary, whether consent is meaningful, and whether people can refuse unnecessary collection without losing access to an essential service. Control also matters after data are collected. A user may agree to one purpose but object when the same information is combined with other records, retained for many years, or shared with another organization. Clear rules about deletion, access, and secondary use can therefore affect whether consent remains meaningful over time. They can also strengthen user trust.


A balanced policy may therefore distinguish between types of data and types of use. Highly sensitive information might require stronger protection, while low-risk information can sometimes be used with lighter restrictions. The appropriate rule depends on the possible harm, the purpose of collection, and the alternatives available.

## 語法・判断整理

LEXG184｜argue の前置詞
with = 議論する相手
about = 議論のテーマ
for = 賛成して主張
against = 反対して主張


Language Focus
1. C for
stricter privacy rules に賛成する立場です。


2. A about
how long data should be stored が論点です。


3. B against
unrestricted collection に反対する立場です。


G02｜Claim Strength
4. may
「一部の利用はriskを生み得る」であり、必ず生むとは限りません。


5. should
必要以上のデータを集めないという規範的提案です。


6. should
high risk という条件を置いた上で、stronger protectionを推奨しています。


Why this wording?
argue for / against は「どちらの立場か」を一語で示せます。
argue about は賛否を決めず、論点そのものを議論しているだけです。
また、may と should を区別すると「起こり得ること」と「そうすべきこと」を混同しにくくなります。

## 解説補強｜Core recap

### 核
trade-offは二択にせず、**risk level → safeguard → conditional decision** で処理する

### 模範解答の読み方
limited useを認めたうえでpurpose説明・拒否可能性・sensitive dataへの強い保護を条件としている

### 次へ
事実の可能性を示す **may / can** と、判断を示す **should** を同じ強さで使わない
