E155｜共同配送ロッカー：混合資料から改善案を書く
P3 / LT03 / SS05 / D03 / RQ13 / WF04 / WT03 / TF11
今日の狙い
利用率の低さを単純な「認知不足」と決めつけず、配置・受取時間・容量の資料から主要障壁を特定して改善案を書く。
時間配分

Service Note
A town installed shared parcel lockers at three public locations to reduce repeated home-delivery attempts. Residents could choose a locker when ordering from participating stores and collect parcels with a code.
After three months, total use was lower than expected. The town first considered increasing advertising. Interviews, however, suggested that many residents already knew the service existed. The more important issue was whether a locker fit the user’s daily route and whether space was available when a parcel arrived.
Operating Rules
• Parcels may remain in a locker for up to 48 hours.
• If all compartments are full, the parcel is sent to the nearest staffed pickup counter.
• Refrigerated items are not accepted.
• Users receive a message when the parcel is placed in a locker.
• Lockers are accessible only during the opening hours of the host facility.
Table
Location | Compartments | Average occupancy at 18:00 | Failed placement rate | Host closing time
Station | 40 | 86% | 12% | 23:00
Library | 30 | 58% | 5% | 20:00
Community Center | 20 | 42% | 3% | 18:00
User Comments
A: “The station locker is useful because I pass it every evening, but it is sometimes full.”
B: “The community center is close to my home, but it closes before I return from work.”
C: “I knew about the library locker, but it is not on my normal route.”
Questions
Q1｜RQ13
What two different barriers are visible across the sources?
Q2｜D03
Why is more advertising unlikely to solve the main problem by itself?
Q3｜RQ13
Which location has the clearest capacity problem? Which has the clearest access-time problem?
Proposal Writing
In 100–120 English words, recommend one priority improvement. Use at least two sources and explain one trade-off or limitation.

解答・解説・自己修正
Q1｜解答例
One barrier is capacity, especially at the station where occupancy and failed placement are high. Another is practical access, including closing time and whether the location fits users’ normal routes.
Q2｜解答例
Many residents already know about the lockers, while the main obstacles concern availability and convenience rather than awareness.
Q3｜解答例
The station has the clearest capacity problem. The community center has the clearest access-time problem.
D03
problem → mechanism → targeted change
の順で書く。利用率が低いという結果だけでは、解決策は決められない。
Model writing
The town should first increase locker capacity at the station rather than spend more on general advertising. The station already has the highest evening occupancy and the highest failed placement rate, while User A confirms that many residents find the location convenient. Adding compartments there would therefore address a demonstrated capacity problem. However, this change would not solve access problems at other sites, such as the community center’s early closing time. The town should evaluate whether failed placements fall after expansion before deciding whether additional lockers or different locations are needed elsewhere. This would separate capacity limits from location or timing problems.
## 解説の考え方

### この問題で考えること
低利用という結果から原因を決めず、**capacity / access time / route convenience** のどこがbottleneckかを資料で特定する。

### 考え方
Station は high occupancy + failed placement + convenient route なので demand はあるが capacity が詰まっている。Community Center は low occupancy でも early closing がaccessを妨げる。Library は awareness があってもroute mismatchがある。したがって一つの広告策では異なるmechanismを処理できない。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「利用率が低い → 知られていない → 広告」  
○ 「locationごとに bottleneck を診断し、Stationではcapacity、Community Centerではtime accessを別々に扱う」

### 模範解答の読み方
Model writing は Station を priority に選び、quantitative evidence + user comment で bottleneck を確定し、capacity expansion のmechanismを示す。その後「他地点のaccess問題は残る」と qualification を置き、failed placement の変化で介入効果を検証する。

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
改善案は **problem label ではなく bottleneck に当てる**。同じ低利用でも原因が違えばsolutionも変わる。


Self-correction
「便利そうな案」ではなく、「どの資料がどの障壁を示し、その障壁にどう効くか」を明示する。
到達目安
A：混合資料から障壁を分解し、根拠・限界つき提案へ変換できる。
B：提案は妥当だが、資料との接続が弱い。
C：認知不足など一つの原因へ飛びつく。
