Reading
[1] A coastal research team monitors water temperature at several points along a bay. The original network used two sensors, one near the harbor and one offshore. Both produced reliable measurements, but researchers sometimes disagreed about whether a rapid temperature change represented the entire bay or only a local event.
[2] The team later added four intermediate sensors. The goal was not to make each measurement more accurate. Instead, the denser network was intended to show how a change moved across space.
Dense Sentence
What the additional sensors made possible was not the elimination of measurement uncertainty but the separation, which had previously been difficult when only two locations were observed, of changes that appeared almost simultaneously across the bay from changes that began near one site and spread gradually.
[3] This distinction matters because the two patterns support different explanations. A nearly simultaneous change may reflect a broad environmental shift. A change that starts near the harbor and later appears elsewhere may suggest a local source or transport process. The sensors do not prove a cause by themselves, but they change which explanations remain plausible.
Table
Event | Harbor | Mid-bay | Offshore | Pattern
A | rises 08:00 | rises 08:05 | rises 08:10 | nearly simultaneous
B | rises 08:00 | rises 08:45 | rises 09:30 | spreading outward
C | stable | rises 08:20 | stable | localized mid-bay
D | falls 06:00 | falls 06:02 | falls 06:05 | nearly simultaneous
[4] More sensors also create new problems. A single faulty instrument may now produce a local pattern that looks meaningful. Maintenance costs rise, and researchers must decide whether small differences in timing reflect real processes or ordinary measurement noise.
[5] For this reason, a denser network improves interpretation only when additional observations are compared systematically. More data do not remove uncertainty automatically. Their value comes from revealing spatial and temporal patterns that a sparse network cannot show.

## 解説の考え方

### この問題で考えること
「センサーが増えた＝一つ一つの測定が正確になった」と考えず、**観測点の増加が何の不確実性を減らすのか**を特定する。

### 考え方
追加sensor → spatial / temporal pattern が見える → simultaneous / spreading / localized を区別できる → plausible explanations を絞れる、という chain が中心。ここで改善するのは individual measurement accuracy ではなく **interpretive resolution** であり、cause はまだ仮説のまま。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「データ点が多いほど原因が分かる」  
○ 「データ点が多いとpatternを区別しやすいが、fault / noise / alternative cause は残る」  
改善点は、**data quantity → pattern discrimination → narrower hypotheses** の中間段階を残すこと。

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
観測を増やしたときは、「精度が上がる」ではなく **何を見分けられるようになったか**を説明する。
