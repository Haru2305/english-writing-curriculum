E154｜外来予約の待ち時間：本文・表・FAQから原因を推論する
P3 / LT03 / D04 / RQ09 / RQ13 / RF03 / SS05 / TF02
今日の狙い
待ち時間という結果を単一原因で説明せず、予約時刻・診療時間のばらつき・遅刻・緊急対応の連鎖を資料から推論する。
時間配分
Silent Plan 3分 / Article 10分 / FAQ 6分 / Table 7分 / Questions 10分 / Japanese Explanation 11分 / Improvement Task 5分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 5分 ＝ Core 60分
Article
A fictional outpatient clinic received complaints that appointments scheduled for late morning often started behind time. Administrators first assumed that too many patients were booked. A review showed a more complicated pattern.
Some early appointments finished faster than expected, but others required additional discussion or tests. A delay created at 9:30 could therefore continue into later slots even when no extra patients were added. The effect became larger when several long consultations occurred in sequence.
Late arrival created a different problem. The clinic sometimes moved a late patient to the next available gap rather than cancelling the visit. This reduced missed care but made the order of later appointments less predictable.
FAQ
Q: Does every patient receive the same appointment length?
A: No. The standard slot is the same, but actual consultation time varies with clinical needs.
Q: Are urgent patients added to the schedule?
A: Sometimes. Clinically urgent cases may be seen earlier than their original position.
Q: Does a 10:00 appointment guarantee that consultation begins exactly at 10:00?
A: No. It is a scheduled target, and delays may accumulate from earlier visits.
Table
Period | Average start delay | Long consultations | Late arrivals | Urgent additions
09:00–10:00 | 6 min | 3 | 2 | 0
10:00–11:00 | 14 min | 6 | 3 | 1
11:00–12:00 | 23 min | 7 | 5 | 2
13:00–14:00 | 8 min | 2 | 2 | 1

解答・解説・自己修正
Q1｜解答例
Several consultations may run longer than their scheduled slots, creating an early delay. Later arrivals and urgent cases can then alter the order further, allowing the delay to accumulate into late morning.
Q2｜解答例
The table shows average delay increasing from 6 to 14 to 23 minutes across the morning, while long consultations and schedule disruptions also become more frequent.
Q3｜解答例
Because delays arise not only from the number of bookings but also from variation in consultation length, late arrivals, urgent additions, and accumulated earlier delays.
Q4｜解答例
遅刻患者を診療中止にせず次の空きへ移すと、その患者の診療自体は維持できる一方、後続患者の順番が変わり、予定時刻との差が広がる場合がある。
Improvement example
Send patients a live delay estimate or updated expected start window when accumulated delays exceed a threshold.
D04
原因は
long consultation → accumulated delay → schedule reordering → larger later delay
のように中間段階を残す。
## 解説の考え方

### この問題で考えること
待ち時間という一つのoutcomeから単一原因へ飛ばず、**variation が時間を通じて累積する仕組み**を作る。

### 考え方
long consultations がslotを超える → early delay が生じる → late arrivals / urgent additions で順序が変わる → 後続slotへ遅れが持ち越される、という chain。Table はこの説明と整合するが、各要因の因果寄与を単独で証明してはいない。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「11時台が遅いから予約数が多すぎる」  
○ 「consultation-time variation とschedule disruption が累積し、late morningでdelayが大きくなる可能性がある」  
改善点は、**列挙した原因を時間方向のmechanismへ接続する**こと。

### 改善案の考え方
live delay estimate は診療時間を無理に均一化する案ではない。**unavoidable variation がある前提で、患者側のuncertaintyを減らす** intervention である。

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
queue / delay 問題では、平均量だけでなく **ばらつき・累積・割り込み・順序変更**を見る。
