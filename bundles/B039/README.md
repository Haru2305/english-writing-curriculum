# B039 — Tsukuba P4 Days 25–30

## Purpose

Finish P4 by converting the full-set process into a repeatable exam routine. B039 adds no major new technique. It diagnoses the last unstable processes, corrects them locally, locks the exam route, rehearses the final procedure, and ends with the P4 Final Gate.

## Sequence

- E229 / Day 25 / 120 min: baseline the repeatability of the full-set routine
- E230 / Day 26 / 120 min: correct strict-character Japanese response weakness
- E231 / Day 27 / 120 min: correct late Q3A/Q3B accuracy under fatigue
- E232 / Day 28 / 120 min: lock answer order and absolute section stop-times
- E233 / Day 29 / 120 min: pre-exam rehearsal with no major strategy changes
- E234 / Day 30 / 120 min: P4 Final Gate — reproduce the 30-day exam process in one sitting

Design: TPL-v4-P4-TSK.

Final routine:
1. choose the pre-decided route before starting
2. obey hard section stop-times
3. use the 2:30 stop-loss rule on stalled short items
4. keep every Japanese response inside its character limit
5. complete Q3A before counting designated positions
6. complete Q3B with position, reason, concrete example, and condition/conclusion
7. protect at least five minutes of final review
8. finish all sections inside 120 minutes

P4 is complete at E234, subject to the existing revalidation gate for the 2027 detailed official admissions guidelines.
