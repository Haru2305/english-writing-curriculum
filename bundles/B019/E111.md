E111｜地域スポーツの参加調査：this / they / such が何を受けるか

P3 / LT01 / A03 / RQ03 / D02 / TF18



今日の狙い

代名詞・this・suchが単語一つではなく、前文の命題や条件全体を受ける場合を追う。指示関係を取ったうえで、具体例が一般論のどこを支えているか判断する。



時間配分

Reference Guide 5分 / Reading 18分 / Questions 14分 / Reference Map 8分 / Short Output 6分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 6分 ＝ Core 60分



Reference Guide

指示語の候補は「直前の名詞」と決めない。

1. 文法上一致するか

2. 意味がつながるか

3. その段落の論理に合うか

4. this / such が前文全体を受けていないか

を確認する。



Reading

[1] A local sports program wants to increase participation among adults who rarely exercise. Organizers first considered lowering the fee, assuming that cost was the main barrier. A survey, however, found that many nonparticipants were more concerned about skill level and embarrassment than price.



[2] This changed the design of the program. Instead of advertising only a cheaper membership, the center created beginner-only sessions and made clear that no previous experience was expected. The fee was reduced slightly, but the larger change was social rather than financial.



[3] Early participation increased, yet the increase did not occur equally across all sessions. Morning sessions attracted many older adults, while evening sessions attracted more people working full-time. This pattern suggested that one program could contain several access barriers at once. Removing the fear of being inexperienced did not remove schedule constraints.



[4] To examine this, organizers added a short question asking new participants why they had chosen a particular session time. They found that many evening participants had wanted to join earlier but could not attend during the day. Such responses helped the center distinguish between “interest in exercise” and “ability to attend a specific session.”



[5] A second example came from participants who stopped attending after two or three visits. Some reported that they still felt uncomfortable once the group included people with very different ability levels. This did not mean the beginner program had failed. It showed that reducing the first barrier to entry and supporting continued participation were different tasks.



[6] The broader lesson is that participation data should be interpreted as part of a process. A higher number of first visits can show that entry has become easier, but continued attendance may depend on different conditions. Programs become more useful when organizers ask which stage of participation a particular change is designed to affect.



Questions

Q1｜RQ03

What does “This” at the beginning of paragraph 2 refer to?



Q2｜RQ03

What does “this” in paragraph 4 refer to?



Q3｜RQ03

What does “Such responses” refer to?



Q4｜RQ08

What broader point do the two examples in paragraphs 4–5 support?



Short Output

In 50–70 English words, explain why increased first-time participation does not by itself prove that the program has solved every access problem.

解答・解説・自己修正



Reading answers

Q1

It refers to the survey finding that many nonparticipants were more concerned about skill level and embarrassment than price.



Q2

It refers to the idea that one program may contain several access barriers and that schedule constraints may remain after another barrier is reduced.



Q3

It refers to the participants’ explanations of why they chose particular session times, especially inability to attend at other times.



Q4｜解答例

They support the broader claim that participation has stages and multiple barriers: a change can improve entry without solving schedule limits or continued-participation problems.



RQ03｜指示内容

this / such は単語ではなく、finding・claim・前文全体を受けることがある。

「何を指すか」だけでなく、「その指示で次文が何を追加するか」を見る。



D02｜general→specific

一般論：barriers differ by stage

具体例：session time / dropout after a few visits

例は一般論を具体化し、同時に一般化の限界も見せる。



Self-correction

直前の名詞を機械的に選んだ設問は、文全体を入れて意味が通るか再確認する。



到達目安

A：段落間の命題参照を追い、例から一般論へ戻れる。

B：近距離の指示は取れるが、文全体を受けるthisで迷う。

C：指示語＝直前名詞と決め打ちする。
