E203｜記録の欠損をどう扱うか：本文・表・注記から「分からない」を正確に残す

複数資料に欠損がある状況で、推測できること・できないことを分け、日本語統合と本文連動作文まで処理する。

時間配分

Passage
A fictional town archive studies ferry use between 1910 and 1920. Ticket books survive for some years, while weather logs and maintenance records survive more continuously. Researchers want to know whether passenger numbers fell mainly because of bad weather or because a new bridge reduced demand.

Table
Year | Ticket-book coverage | Recorded passengers | Severe-weather days | Bridge status
1911 | 12 months | 42,100 | 18 | not open
1914 | 7 months | 21,900 | 26 | not open
1917 | 12 months | 31,600 | 20 | open
1920 | 5 months | 12,400 | 15 | open

Archive Note
Missing ticket books were not necessarily months with zero ferry use. Some books were lost when records were moved in the 1940s. No complete monthly passenger series survives for 1914 or 1920.

[1] The available numbers therefore cannot be compared as if every year covered twelve months. The lower total in 1920 may partly reflect missing records.

[2] At the same time, complete-year totals for 1911 and 1917 are lower after the bridge opened, which is consistent with reduced ferry demand. Yet those two years also differ in other ways, so the comparison is suggestive rather than conclusive.

[3] Weather may have influenced short-term use, but annual severe-weather counts do not show which missing months were busiest or whether particular storms occurred on high-demand days.

[4] The safest conclusion is not that nothing can be learned. It is that different claims have different evidential support: record loss explains why some totals are incomplete, the complete years suggest a possible bridge effect, and the available weather data are too coarse to isolate their contribution.

Questions
Q1｜RQ03
What does “those two years” in paragraph 2 refer to?

Q2｜RQ13
Why should 1914 and 1920 not be directly compared with 1911 as annual totals?

Q3｜D10
How does paragraph 4 qualify the claim that the evidence is incomplete?

Q4｜RQ09
What is the strongest claim supported about the bridge?

解答・解説・自己修正

Q1｜解答
1911 and 1917.

Q2｜解答例
Because 1914 and 1920 have only seven and five months of surviving ticket records, so their recorded totals are incomplete and are not twelve-month passenger counts.

Q3｜解答例
It says incomplete evidence does not mean the archive is useless; instead, some claims are stronger than others depending on which records survive.

Q4｜解答例
The complete-year comparison is consistent with the bridge reducing ferry demand, but it does not by itself prove that the bridge caused the decline.

Model Japanese synthesis
1914年と1920年は乗船券記録が一部月しか残らず、低い総数を年間利用者数として比較できない。一方、12か月分が残る1911年と1917年では橋開通後に利用が減っており、需要減少を示唆する。ただし他条件も異なる。天候記録は年単位で粗く、欠損月との対応も不明なため、天候の寄与までは分離できない。

Model writing
The next step should be to reconstruct monthly ferry use from independent records, such as newspaper schedules, company accounts, or bridge traffic reports. This would address two major limitations: missing ticket books in 1914 and 1920, and the inability of annual weather counts to show when demand changed. If researchers could estimate monthly ferry activity and match it with bridge use and specific weather events, they could distinguish a gradual decline after the bridge opened from short-term drops caused by storms. The goal should be improved temporal resolution, not simply collecting more annual totals.

Self-correction
missing ≠ zero。unknownを保持しながら、claim strength を資料別に分ける。

## 解説の使い方

### この問題で考えること
欠損があるときの目標は「空白を埋めること」ではなく、**資料ごとに言える範囲を分け、unknown を残したまま次の検証へ進むこと**。

### 考え方
1914・1920は ticket-book coverage が不完全なので、recorded passengers を年間総数として比較できない。  
一方、12か月分が残る1911・1917では橋開通後に低下しており、bridge effect と整合する。ただし二年だけでは他条件を排除できない。weather data も年単位で粗く、欠損月との対応がない。  
したがって、  
**coverage確認 → comparable な資料だけで主張 → coarse data の限界を残す → より細かい独立資料を追加する**  
という順になる。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「欠損が多いので何も分からない」  
× 「記録がない月は利用者ゼロとして埋める」  
○ 「完全資料から言えることは言い、不完全資料からは言えないことを明示する」  
強いのは、**unknown を結論の一部として管理している**点。

### 模範作文の読み方
提案は「資料をもっと集める」では弱い。model は monthly resolution を上げるという目的を置き、新聞・会計・bridge traffic という independent records を使って、**欠損と天候の二つの限界を同時に減らす**設計になっている。

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
欠測アンケート、途切れたセンサーデータ、古い記録、追跡脱落でも、  
**missing ≠ zero / missing ≠ average / missing ≠ random**  
をまず確認し、推測で埋める前に「何が分からないか」を固定する。
