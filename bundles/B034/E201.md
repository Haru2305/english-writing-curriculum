E201｜平均値の改善は誰の改善か：高密度文・表・和訳から分布を読む

P3 / LT03 / A29 / D10 / RQ09 / RQ13 / RF04 / SS03 / TF08

今日の狙い
平均値と分布の変化を分け、高密度文を分解しながら「平均改善＝全員改善」という誤読を防ぐ。

時間配分
Silent Plan 1分 / Reading 17分 / Table 8分 / Dense Sentence 12分 / Questions 10分 / Translation 8分 / Self-check 2分 / Post-solve 2分 ＝ Core 60分

Reading
[1] A fictional tutoring program reports that average test performance increased after six weeks. The mean score rose from 62 to 69, which appears encouraging.

Table
Group | Before | After | Change
Lowest quarter | 38 | 47 | +9
Middle half | 64 | 69 | +5
Highest quarter | 84 | 85 | +1

[2] The table shows that gains were not evenly distributed. Students in the lowest quarter improved more on average, while the highest quarter changed little.

[3] That pattern can matter for interpretation. The same seven-point increase in the overall mean could arise from many different distributions of individual change. Some students may improve substantially while others remain stable or even decline.

Dense Sentence
Although an increase in the overall mean can accurately describe the direction of change for the group as a whole, it cannot by itself establish that the intervention benefited most individuals, because identical shifts in the mean may be produced by substantially different distributions of gains and losses.

[4] This does not make the mean useless. A mean is a compact summary of group performance. The problem is treating a group summary as if it described every member of the group.

[5] A fuller evaluation would examine both the average and the distribution of change, including how many students improved, stayed similar, or declined.

Questions
Q1｜RQ13
Which group contributed the largest average gain?

Q2｜A29
Rewrite the Dense Sentence as three simpler English sentences.

Q3｜D10
What qualification does paragraph 4 add to the criticism of the mean?

Q4｜RQ09
Why can the same mean increase hide different individual outcomes?

Q5｜RQ09
What additional data would best test whether most individuals benefited?

Translation
Translate the Dense Sentence naturally into Japanese.

Self-check
□ I separated group mean from individual change.
□ I retained the usefulness of the mean.
□ I did not infer distribution from one average.

---

解答・解説・自己修正

Q1｜解答
The lowest quarter, with an average increase of nine points.

Q2｜解答例
The group mean can correctly show that overall performance increased. However, that does not prove that most individual students improved. Different combinations of gains and losses can produce the same change in the mean.

Q3｜解答例
It says the mean is still useful as a compact summary; the problem is overextending that summary to every individual.

Q4｜解答例
Because a mean compresses many individual values into one number, and different patterns of improvement, stability, and decline can yield the same group average.

Q5｜解答例
The distribution of individual score changes, including counts or proportions of students who improved, remained similar, or declined.

Translation｜和訳例
全体平均の上昇は集団全体として変化した方向を正しく表しうるが、それだけでは介入が大多数の個人に利益をもたらしたとは言えない。なぜなら、平均値が同じだけ上昇していても、その背後には得点の増減が大きく異なる分布がありうるからである。

Self-correction
aggregate ≠ individual。平均を否定せず、射程を限定する。

到達目安
A：平均・分布・個人の三層を分けて説明できる。
B：平均の限界は分かるが、平均自体を無意味と扱う。
C：平均上昇から全員改善を推論する。
