Context

Universities send students a large amount of information: registration instructions, scholarship notices, safety updates, event announcements, and changes to campus services. Because many messages are long, some students read only the first few lines or postpone reading them.

Automatic summarization may help by placing the required action, deadline, and main condition near the top. However, a summary is still a reduced representation of the original notice. A rare exception, special eligibility rule, or warning may disappear if the system treats it as less important.

For this reason, a useful summary should not replace the original document. It should help students identify what they need to check next. Universities also need to know whether students actually understood the message rather than merely opened it.

Model answer (101 words)

I think universities should provide short AI-generated summaries of long administrative notices, but only as a supplement to the original message. Students often ignore important information when an email is too long, so a clear summary could help them notice deadlines and required actions. However, an automatic summary may omit a condition or exception. For example, a scholarship notice might mention an earlier deadline for international applicants. The university should therefore show the original notice directly below the summary and warn students to check it before making an important decision. This would improve convenience without treating the summary as fully reliable.

## Q3Bの思考回路

E208で導入した6つの問いを、ここでは短く再利用する。

- **答えは？**：AI summaryは使う。ただし original の代替にはしない
- **なぜ？**：長い通知では deadline / required action が埋もれやすい
- **どうして？**：要点を上に出せば、学生が次に確認すべき情報へ到達しやすい
- **具体的には？**：scholarship の例外deadlineのように、summaryが落とし得る情報がある
- **例外は？**：自動要約は reduced representation であり、重要条件を省く可能性がある
- **だから？**：originalを直下に残し、重要判断の前に確認させる

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「AIは便利なので要約を付けるべき」  
○ 「長文通知の見落としを減らせる一方、要約で条件が落ちるので、originalへ戻れる設計を残す」  

本番では理由を増やさず、**benefit → mechanism → risk → safeguard** の一本を約100語に収める。
