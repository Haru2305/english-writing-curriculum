E133｜都市の暑さ対策：文の役割から挿入位置を決める

P3 / LT02 / RF07 空所・挿入 / D08 Review / RQ07 / TF11



今日の狙い

RF07を正式導入する。表面語が近い場所ではなく、前後の論理・照応・段落機能から文の位置を決める。



時間配分

Insertion Guide 6分 / Reading 18分 / Questions 12分 / Insertion Tasks 10分 / Short Explanation 6分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 5分 ＝ Core 60分



Insertion Guide

1. 候補文が前の何を受けるかを見る

2. 候補文が後ろに何を導入するかを見る

3. this / these / such / however などの照応・論理を確認する

4. 同じ単語があるだけでは正解にしない



Reading

[1] Cities often respond to summer heat by adding trees, reflective roofs, shaded walkways, or surfaces that absorb less solar energy. Each measure can reduce heat in some conditions, but a city-wide average does not show whether the people most exposed to heat actually benefit.



[A]



[2] Trees are a clear example. A mature tree can cool a sidewalk through shade and evaporation, yet its effect depends on where people spend time. A tree placed along a wide road may reduce surface temperature without helping residents who wait for buses on an unshaded corner nearby.



[B]



[3] The same problem appears with reflective materials. A lighter roof can reduce heat entering a building, but residents on upper floors may benefit more than people in lower rooms. A bright pavement may also reduce surface temperature while increasing reflected radiation at pedestrian level if the design is poor.



[C]



[4] For this reason, some planners now combine physical measurements with maps of daily activity. They ask where older residents walk, where children wait after school, and which housing blocks have little nighttime cooling. The goal is not to abandon temperature data but to connect it to patterns of exposure.



[D]



[5] A useful heat policy therefore requires two levels of judgment. The city needs measures that reduce heat overall, and it also needs evidence that the reduction occurs where vulnerable people experience it. A technically successful intervention can still be socially weak if its benefits appear in the wrong places.



Candidate Sentence

This distinction matters because a reduction in measured temperature is not automatically the same as a reduction in human exposure to heat.



Questions

Where should the Candidate Sentence be inserted: A, B, C, or D?



Q2｜RQ07

Q3｜RQ08

Insertion Task 2

Choose the best connector for the blank:

a. For example

b. However

c. Therefore

d. Similarly



Short Explanation

解答・解説・自己修正



Q1｜正解

A



理由

Paragraph 1 ends by contrasting city-wide averages with whether exposed people benefit. The Candidate Sentence names exactly that distinction. Paragraph 2 then gives trees as the first concrete example of it. A therefore creates general claim → explicit distinction → example.



Why not B?

B is locally plausible because paragraph 2 discusses exposure, but placing the sentence there would repeat a distinction already illustrated rather than prepare the example.



Q2｜解答例

Paragraph 4 introduces a method for connecting physical temperature measurements with where vulnerable people actually spend time.



Q3｜解答例

Heat policies should be judged not only by average cooling but also by whether cooling reaches the places and people most exposed.

b. However



RF07｜空所・挿入

正解位置は「前だけ」「後ろだけ」では決めない。

before → inserted sentence → after

の三点セットで意味・照応・機能がつながるかを見る。



Model explanation

The sentence fits at A because paragraph 1 has just contrasted average cooling with actual benefit to exposed people. The sentence names that contrast, and paragraph 2 immediately illustrates it with trees. At B, the idea would arrive after the example had already begun, so it would function more like repetition than a bridge.



Self-correction

正解候補を見つけた後も、最低1つの別候補と比較する。「入る」ではなく「ここが最も機能する」を説明する。



到達目安

A：前後機能と照応から唯一の位置を説明できる。

B：正解できるが、根拠が単語一致に寄る。

C：近い語がある場所へ機械的に挿入する。
