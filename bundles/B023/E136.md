E136｜海岸保全の研究：段落の機能を保って空所を復元する

P3 / LT03 / RF07 Transfer / RQ07 / D05 Review / TF08



今日の狙い

段落全体の役割から空所文を選ぶ。文単位の自然さだけでなく、段落が「主張・譲歩・根拠・限定」のどこにいるかを使う。



時間配分

Paragraph Function Map 5分 / Reading 18分 / Questions 10分 / Gap Tasks 14分 / Function Explanation 5分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 5分 ＝ Core 60分



Reading

[1] Coastal towns sometimes restore dunes by planting vegetation and limiting access to fragile areas. These measures can reduce erosion because plant roots help hold sand in place. They are also relatively visible to residents, which can make the project easier to explain than an underground engineering intervention.



[2] A three-year monitoring project compared several restored sites with nearby untreated sites. At most restored sites, dune height increased and vegetation cover expanded. Storm damage was also lower in areas where the dune had enough width to absorb wave energy.



[3] [Blank A]



[4] The researchers therefore warned against treating vegetation as a universal solution. In narrow sites, there may not be enough space for a dune to develop. In heavily visited areas, repeated trampling can damage new plants unless access routes are redesigned. Some locations may still require seawalls or other structures when buildings sit very close to the shore.



[5] This qualification does not cancel the value of restoration. Rather, it changes the question from “Does planting work?” to “Under what conditions does planting contribute enough protection to justify the space and maintenance it requires?” That question allows natural restoration and engineered protection to be compared on a common basis.



[6] The broader lesson is that environmental interventions should be evaluated by mechanism and local conditions, not by whether they fit a preferred category such as “natural” or “engineered.” A measure can be environmentally attractive and still be unsuitable at a particular site.



Candidates for Blank A

a. Residents generally preferred vegetation because it looked more natural than concrete.

b. However, the positive average concealed large differences among sites.

c. Therefore, every town should replace hard coastal structures with dunes.

d. For example, the project used drones to photograph the coastline every month.

解答・解説・自己修正



Q1｜正解

b. However, the positive average concealed large differences among sites.



理由

Paragraph 2 gives positive results. Paragraph 4 begins “therefore” and introduces limits by site. Paragraph 3 must pivot from average success to variation. Option b performs exactly that bridge.



Q2｜解答例

It must qualify the positive findings by introducing site-to-site variation, preparing the conditions and limits discussed in paragraph 4.



Q3｜解答例

The author concedes that restoration can work and has real value, but maintains that its effectiveness depends on local conditions and mechanism rather than on the label “natural.”



Gap Task 2

b. The useful comparison is therefore not between labels but between conditions, mechanisms, and trade-offs.



RF07 Transfer

良い選択肢でも、段落の役割に合わなければ不正解。

local fit と global fit を分けて見る。



Model function explanation

Option a is plausible because the passage mentions that residents may find vegetation attractive. However, paragraph 3 must prepare the shift from positive average results to limits across sites. A preference statement does not create that transition, so it fits the topic locally but fails the paragraph’s structural role.
