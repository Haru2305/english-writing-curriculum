E135｜地域交通の案内：照応と既知→新情報で文を挿入する

P3 / LT03 / RF07 Guided / D08 Review / RQ03 / SS04 / TF11



今日の狙い

文挿入で照応と情報構造を使う。this / these / such / the change などが何を受け、次に何を新情報として運ぶかを確認する。



時間配分

Reference Flow 5分 / Source Reading 16分 / Questions 10分 / Insertion Tasks 14分 / Short Explanation 7分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 5分 ＝ Core 60分



Source A｜Transit Notice

A town introduced an on-demand evening bus after regular services ended. Passengers could request a ride by phone or through a simple web form. During the first month, the town received many questions about whether the service followed a fixed route.



[1] The vehicle did not follow one permanent route. Instead, requests made before a cutoff time were grouped so that several passengers could share part of a journey. [A] This arrangement reduced empty travel, but it also meant that pickup times could vary from day to day.



[2] At first, the notice explained the booking deadline but did not explain why it mattered. Some passengers assumed that a request made five minutes before departure could be handled in the same way as one made an hour earlier. [B] The town therefore added a short explanation: earlier requests gave the operator more time to combine trips efficiently.



[3] A second problem involved the phrase “nearest pickup point.” Some residents interpreted it as the nearest ordinary bus stop. In fact, the service sometimes used community centers or well-lit street corners instead. [C] These locations were chosen to keep detours manageable while avoiding places where passengers would have to wait alone in poorly lit areas.



[4] After the notice was revised, fewer users called to ask about routes or pickup points. [D] The change did not make the service perfectly predictable, but it made the relationship between booking rules and service design easier to understand.



Candidate Sentence

This flexibility was possible because the route was created only after the day’s requests had been collected.



Questions

Q1｜RF07

Where should the Candidate Sentence be inserted: A, B, C, or D?



Q2｜RQ03

What does “These locations” refer to in paragraph 3?



Q3｜D08

Why does the phrase “This flexibility” work at the correct position?



Insertion Task 2

Insert the following sentence at the best position in paragraph 2:

“Without that explanation, the deadline looked like an arbitrary restriction rather than part of the routing process.”



Short Explanation

In 45–65 English words, explain how reference and old→new information determine one of your insertion choices.



Self-check

□ I found the referent before choosing the slot.

□ I checked whether old information becomes the subject of the inserted sentence.

□ I checked what new information the sentence prepares.

□ I did not treat repeated words as sufficient evidence.

解答・解説・自己修正



Q1｜正解

A



理由

“This flexibility” refers to the route not being fixed. The Candidate Sentence explains how that flexibility is possible, and the following sentence gives its operational consequence. old information → explanation → consequence の流れになる。



Q2｜解答

Community centers or well-lit street corners used as pickup points.



Q3｜解答例

It turns the previously introduced idea of a non-fixed route into the topic of the next sentence, then adds new information about why such flexibility is possible.



Insertion Task 2

The best position is after “Some passengers assumed that a request made five minutes before departure could be handled in the same way as one made an hour earlier.” It explains the misunderstanding before the town’s response.


別解：①最初の文（did not explain why it mattered）の直後も可。②段落末（“The town therefore added a short explanation: …” の直後）も可で、この場合 that explanation は直前の a short explanation を指し、指示関係はいちばん明確になります。標準解は、直後に「The town therefore added」が続き、問題→結果→対応の順になる位置です。



RF07 × D08

挿入文の主語が this / these / the change なら、その語が指す内容は原則として直前までに導入されている必要がある。

old → inserted bridge → new

の流れを追う。



Model explanation

The sentence belongs after the passengers’ mistaken assumption because “that explanation” refers to the reasoning the notice lacked, and the example just given shows the result of that gap. The next sentence, “The town therefore added a short explanation,” then follows as a response. Placing the sentence later would break the problem-to-response sequence.



Self-correction

候補文の最初の名詞句・指示語と最後の情報に線を引き、前後のどちらへ接続するか確認する。



到達目安

A：照応と情報構造の両方から位置を説明できる。

B：照応は取れるが、次文との接続説明が弱い。

C：this/theseの参照先を曖昧なまま選ぶ。
