E069｜高齢者支援の連携：誰を主語にするかで説明の流れを保つ
P2 / LT03 / A13・D08 Transfer / TF03
今日の狙い
A13/D08を別題材へTransferし、複数の主体が登場する説明文でもold→newの受け渡しを保って読む。主語の切替が情報の焦点をどう変えるかを説明する。
時間配分
Flow Plan 5分 / Reading 17分 / Reference & Flow 11分 / Questions 10分 / Rewrite 7分 / Self-check 4分 / Post-solve 6分 ＝ Core 60分
Flow Plan
複数の人・組織が出る文章では、
1. 今のtopicは誰か
2. 次文の主語は何を受けるか
3. 新しい主体を出す必要があるか
を確認する。能動/受動は情報の焦点を調整する道具。
Reading
[1] Support for older people often involves several services rather than one institution. A person may receive medical care from a clinic, help with daily activities from a home-care worker, and practical support from family members or community groups. Each service can work well on its own and still fail to form a coherent system if important information does not move between them.
[2] Consider a change in mobility. A family member may notice that an older person has begun avoiding stairs. That observation can affect decisions about medication, home equipment, transport, or exercise. If the information remains only within the family, however, other services may continue to act on an older picture of the person’s needs. The problem is not necessarily lack of effort; it is a break in the information chain.
[3] Coordination can reduce this gap, but coordination does not mean that every detail should be shared with everyone. Information should be relevant to the task and handled with appropriate consent and privacy protections. A care worker may need to know that walking has become difficult without needing access to unrelated medical details. Clear roles can therefore improve both information flow and limits on information flow.
[4] These limits are easier to manage when responsibility for communication is explicit. If everyone assumes that someone else will report a change, no one may do so. A named contact or agreed reporting route gives new information somewhere to go. The information can then be passed to the people who need it, while unnecessary details remain restricted.
[5] Good coordination is therefore not simply maximum sharing. It is the controlled movement of relevant information across services so that decisions remain connected to the person’s current situation.
[6] The same information-flow problem can appear during transitions between services. A person leaving a hospital may have new instructions about mobility, meals, or medication. Those instructions are useful only if the people supporting the person at home know which parts affect their responsibilities. A discharge document can contain all the necessary facts and still fail as communication if relevant details are buried or the receiving service is unclear about what action follows from them. Coordination therefore depends on both accurate information and a visible route from information to responsibility.
Reference & Flow
Paragraph 4で、
“These limits” → “responsibility for communication” → “A named contact” → “The information”
がどのようにold→newでつながるか説明しなさい。

解答・解説・自己修正
Reference & Flow｜例
“These limits” receives paragraph 3’s limits on sharing. The sentence adds a new issue: responsibility for communication. “A named contact” then provides a solution to that issue. “The information” becomes the established topic and is carried into the passive sentence about where it goes.
Reading answers
Q1
“These limits” connects paragraph 4 directly to the privacy/relevance limits established in paragraph 3, so the paragraph begins with known information.
Q2
B keeps “the information” as the topic established in the preceding sentence. The passive form allows the paragraph to continue following the information rather than switching focus to staff.
Q3｜B
relevant information / clear limits / explicit responsibility の3要素をまとめる。
Q4｜解答例
Services can act on different or outdated information if changes are not communicated across them, so individually good decisions may not form a coherent plan.
A13/D08 Transfer
E063〜E064で学んだold→newと主語選択を、複数主体が登場するcare coordinationへ転用した。文法的な能動/受動の正誤ではなく、どのtopicを維持するかで判断する。
Rewrite｜例
“Staff can then pass the information to the people who need it.”
焦点がinformationの移動から、staffという行為者へ移る。文脈によっては正しいが、元の段落ではtopic continuityが弱くなる。
Self-correction
主語の選択理由を「受動態だから」ではなく、「前文の何をold informationとして受けるか」で説明する。
到達目安
A：複数主体がいてもtopic chainを追い、主語選択の効果を説明できる。
B：照応は追えるが、情報構造として説明しにくい。
C：各文を独立して読み、段落のtopic continuityを失う。
