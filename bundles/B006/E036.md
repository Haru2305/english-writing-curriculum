Text A｜Treatment decision
A patient has two medically reasonable treatment options. One is more intensive and may produce a faster improvement, but it also has a higher rate of short-term side effects. The other has a slower expected effect but allows the patient to continue working during treatment. The clinician selects both as reasonable options and explains the expected trade-offs. The patient prefers maintaining daily activity and chooses the less intensive option. The clinician acknowledges that no option is clearly best for every person and records the patient's informed preference rather than treating the choice as a rejection of medical advice. Before the decision is finalized, the patient explains in their own words why the slower option fits better. The clinician corrects one misunderstanding about recovery time and confirms that the patient still prefers the same treatment after the clarification.


Text B｜Community sports budget
A town has enough money either to renovate an old sports center or to expand a small public-health program this year. Residents disagree about which project should receive priority. Some favor the sports center because it provides exercise space and community activities. Others argue that the health program reaches people who are less likely to use sports facilities. The council recognizes that both projects have value but cannot fund both immediately. It prioritizes the health program this year and delays the renovation, while publishing the criteria used for the decision. The council also commits to reviewing participation data and community need before the next budget cycle. This makes the priority temporary rather than treating the delayed project as permanently unimportant. Residents are invited to comment on the published criteria, and the council explains that prioritizing one project this year does not mean the other has no public value.


Text C｜Early treatment study
An early study of a new therapy reports encouraging results, but the trial includes relatively few participants and follows them for only six months. Researchers acknowledge that uncommon adverse effects may not yet have appeared. They also recognize that the observed benefit could justify a larger study. Later, the team discovers a coding error that slightly changes one outcome. The researchers admit the error, correct the analysis, and explain that the overall pattern remains similar but somewhat less certain. The updated report does not present the treatment as either completely proven or entirely worthless at this very early stage of evidence. Instead, the team separates what remains encouraging from what has become more uncertain. It proposes a larger follow-up study with longer observation and states in advance which outcomes will be used to judge whether the therapy provides a meaningful benefit. Clinicians reading the report are reminded that the early finding may inform discussion but should not be presented to patients as a guaranteed result.

Short Writing
モデル答案
A health system may prioritize one program when resources are limited, but it should acknowledge the value of the option that is delayed. Decision makers should recognize the opportunity cost of the priority rather than treating the rejected project as worthless. Clear criteria make the choice easier to explain and evaluate.


別解
Researchers can recognize that a new treatment is promising while acknowledging important uncertainty. If they discover a specific mistake, they should admit the error and correct the analysis. Choosing to continue research does not require pretending that the current evidence is complete.

解説補強｜Checkpointで「選ぶ」と「優先する」を分ける

### このCheckpointで考えること
B006では、decisionをすべて同じ「選択」として扱わないことが重要です。

### 共通する3種類のdecision
1. **personal choice**：本人が価値観で選ぶ
2. **system priority**：scarce resourceをcriterionで優先する
3. **evidence judgment**：limitationを認めてclaimの強さを決める

### 弱い考え方との比較
× chosen = objectively best
× prioritized = alternatives have no value
× limitation = evidence worthless

○ choiceはpreference、priorityはresource constraint、limitationはclaim boundaryを表します。

### Short Writingの組み立て
**decision type → relevant criterion → trade-off / limitation → justified conclusion** の順で書きます。

まず「誰が、何を基準に、何を制約として決めるdecisionか」を特定してから英語表現を選びます。
