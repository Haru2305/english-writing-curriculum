Reading
Health systems have limited staff, time, beds, and money. When demand exceeds available capacity, decision makers must decide what to prioritize. This does not mean that less urgent needs have no value. It means that not every need can receive the same level of attention at the same time.


Individual preferences and system priorities can differ. A patient may prefer a nearby clinic to a larger hospital because travel is easier. A clinician may favor a treatment pathway that reduces unnecessary admissions. A health system may prioritize urgent cases when beds or staff are temporarily limited.


Prevention creates a longer-term challenge. Resources used for vaccination, screening, or health education may not produce an immediate visible result. However, preventive programs can reduce future demand. A system that prioritizes only current emergencies may therefore overlook investments that lower risk later.


Fairness also matters. Prioritizing those who are easiest to treat could improve some short-term statistics while leaving people with greater barriers behind. On the other hand, giving every person exactly the same amount of time or money may be impossible or inefficient. Priorities can also change as circumstances change. During a temporary shortage, urgency may dominate, while long-term planning may place greater weight on prevention or access. Measuring the result matters because a rule that looks reasonable in theory can produce unexpected disadvantages in practice.


Good prioritization requires an explicit reason. Decision makers should explain whether they are prioritizing urgency, expected benefit, prevention, access, or another goal. Without a clear criterion, a priority can look arbitrary even when resources are genuinely limited.

解説補強｜preferenceとpriorityを混ぜない

### この問題で考えること
個人が「好き・望む」ことと、systemが限られたresourceを「先に配る」ことは別です。**priorityにはcriterionとopportunity costが必要**です。

### 考え方
limited capacity
→ not every need can be served immediately
→ urgency / expected benefit / fairness などのcriterionを選ぶ
→ one need is prioritized over another
→ delayed need remains valuable even if not first

### 弱い考え方との比較
× The system should prioritize what most people prefer.
○ A system may prioritize urgent or high-benefit cases even when individual preferences differ.

### 模範解答の読み方
prefer は個人の価値、favor はpolicy support、prioritize はscarce resourceの配分です。主語が変わると、判断の種類も変わります。

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
resource allocationでは **whose preference / which criterion / what is sacrificed** を分けます。
