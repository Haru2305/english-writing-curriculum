Reading
Research papers often contain a section on limitations. Some readers may think that acknowledging limitations makes a study look weak, but the opposite can be true. A clear limitation helps readers understand how far a conclusion can reasonably be taken.


A small sample, short follow-up period, or limited study population does not automatically make a result meaningless. It may mean that the finding should not be generalized too broadly. Researchers can acknowledge this boundary while still explaining why the result is useful.


Errors require a different response. If researchers discover that a calculation was wrong, they may need to admit the error and correct it. Admitting an error is stronger than simply acknowledging uncertainty because it accepts that a specific part of the work was incorrect.


Researchers may also recognize needs that follow from the study. A result may suggest that a larger trial, a different population, or longer observation is necessary. Recognizing the need for more evidence does not mean rejecting the current findings; it identifies what remains unresolved.


Scientific credibility therefore depends partly on how researchers handle inconvenient information. A paper that hides every weakness may sound confident but give readers a misleading picture. A paper that clearly acknowledges limitations can support a more accurate interpretation of its evidence. Transparency can also make later comparison easier. If researchers clearly state which outcomes were planned in advance and which analyses were added later, readers can distinguish confirmatory evidence from exploratory findings. This does not make exploratory analysis inherently unscientific; it simply changes how strongly the result should be interpreted.

Why these words?
acknowledgeは「問題があることを認める」のであって、その問題が全てを無効にすると同意することではありません。
admitはerrorなど、より自分に不利な具体的事実で自然です。
recognizeはimportance / need / valueを「認識する」時に適します。

### この問題で考えること
limitationの記載は研究を否定するためではなく、**どこまでなら結論を信頼できるか境界を示すため**です。

### 考え方
finding
→ sample / design / follow-up limitationをacknowledge
→ conclusionのscopeを限定
→ replication / further studyのneedをrecognize
→ specific mistakeならadmitして修正

### 弱い考え方との比較
× If a study has limitations, its result is useless.
○ A study can remain informative while its conclusions are limited to the evidence actually available.

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
研究解釈では **limitation / error / uncertainty / need for further evidence** を別カテゴリーにします。
