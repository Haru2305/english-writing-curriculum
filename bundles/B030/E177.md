E177｜学習支援の二制度：自由利用と課題限定利用を比較して評価する
P3 / LT03 / RQ12 / RF05 / D06 / WF05 / WT03 / TF05

今日の狙い
デジタル学習支援を自由利用にする制度と課題限定で使わせる制度を、アクセス・依存・公平性・教員負担の共通軸で比較し、要約から条件付き評価へつなげる。

時間配分
Silent Plan 1分 / Passage A 10分 / Passage B 10分 / Questions 10分 / English Summary 10分 / Evaluation Writing 11分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 5分 ＝ Core 60分

Passage A
A university is considering allowing students to use a digital study-support tool freely in all courses. The tool can explain terminology, suggest practice questions, and provide feedback on draft outlines. Supporters argue that open access lets students seek help at the moment they need it and reduces differences between students who can and cannot obtain private tutoring.

The concern is that unrestricted use may blur the line between support and substitution. Some students may rely on generated explanations before trying to solve a problem themselves. Instructors may also find it harder to know which parts of an assignment reflect the student’s independent work.

Passage B
A second proposal allows the same tool only in designated activities. Instructors identify tasks where support is permitted, such as vocabulary review or practice planning, while assessed writing and problem solving remain tool-free unless explicitly stated otherwise.

This approach makes expectations clearer and may preserve more independent practice. However, the rules can vary across courses, creating confusion. Students may also lose useful support in situations where instructors did not anticipate a need, and teaching staff must spend time defining and updating permitted uses.

Questions
Q1｜RQ12
What goal is shared by both proposals?

Q2｜D06
Compare the proposals on accessibility and clarity of rules.

Q3｜RQ09
Why might unrestricted use affect instructors’ interpretation of student performance?

Q4｜RQ10
Why is task-limited use not automatically simpler for students?

English Summary
Summarize both passages in 70–90 English words using at least two shared comparison axes.

Evaluation Writing
In 90–110 English words, recommend a policy for a university that wants broad access to study support while still assessing students’ independent ability. Include two criteria and one qualification.

解答・解説・自己修正

Q1｜解答例
Both proposals aim to give students useful learning support while preserving meaningful independent learning and assessment.

Q2｜解答例
Free use maximizes immediate access but creates less certainty about how the tool was used in assessed work. Task-limited use gives clearer boundaries within each activity but may create inconsistent rules across courses.

Q3｜解答例
If students rely heavily on the tool, submitted work may no longer show the same amount of independent reasoning, making performance harder to interpret.

Q4｜解答例
Because permitted uses may differ between courses, and students must learn several sets of rules rather than one general policy.

Model English summary
Both proposals provide digital learning support but balance access and control differently. Free use gives students help whenever needed and may reduce unequal access to tutoring, yet it can encourage dependence and make assessed performance harder to interpret. Task-limited use creates clearer boundaries for independent work, but rules may vary across courses and require additional staff planning. The central trade-off is broad access versus predictable limits on supported work.

Model evaluation
The university should allow broad use for ungraded study while requiring clear course-level rules for assessed tasks. Students could use the tool for terminology, planning, and practice, but instructors should specify when submitted work must be completed independently. This preserves access to support while making assessment expectations easier to interpret. However, the university should keep the rule system simple and consistent across courses where possible; otherwise students may spend more effort understanding policy differences than using the support effectively.

## 解説の考え方

### この問題で考えること
「自由利用か制限利用か」の好みを述べる問題ではない。**access を広く保ちながら、independent ability を測れる状態をどう残すか**を、同じ比較軸で判断する。

### 考え方
まず access / assessment validity / rule clarity / staff workload を両制度に同じ順で当てる。そのうえで、全面自由か全面禁止かという二択にせず、**ungraded study では広く使い、assessed task では境界を明示する**という制度設計を作る。これなら access の便益を残しつつ、提出物が本人の独立した能力をどこまで示すかを読み取りやすくできる。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「自由利用は便利だからよい」  
× 「制限すれば不正を防げるからよい」  
○ 「自由利用は access を改善する一方、評価対象まで代替すると performance の解釈が難しくなる。したがって用途ごとに境界を分ける」  
改善点は、抽象的な長所短所を並べるのでなく、**どの criterion が、どの mechanism で判断を左右すか**を示していること。

### 答案の組み立て
- **Position**: ungraded study は広く許可し、assessed task には明確なルールを置く
- **Reason**: access と assessment validity を両立しやすい
- **Mechanism**: 支援用途を残しつつ、独立能力を測る場面では代替を制限できる
- **Support**: terminology / planning / practice と submitted work を分ける
- **Qualification**: course ごとの差が増えすぎないようルールを簡潔・一貫化する
- **Return**: selective use を制度化する

### 模範解答の読み方
Model evaluation は、1文目で方針、2文目で境界の具体化、3文目で access と assessment の両立 mechanism、4文目で rule complexity という qualification を置いている。最後の文は「制限を増やせばよい」という方向へ行き過ぎないための戻しになっている。

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
技術導入は「使う / 使わない」ではなく、**どの用途では便益が大きく、どの用途では人間の独立判断や責任を残すべきか**に分解すると論じやすい。
