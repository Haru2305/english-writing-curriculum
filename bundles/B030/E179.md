E179｜再利用品シェア拠点：混合資料から廃棄削減とアクセス改善を提案する
P3 / LT03 / SS05 / D03 / RQ13 / WF04 / WT03 / TF10

今日の狙い
再利用率の低さを「提供量不足」だけで説明せず、受取時間・保管容量・需要とのずれを資料から特定し、優先改善案を書く。

時間配分
Silent Plan 1分 / Service Note 9分 / Rules 5分 / Table 8分 / User Comments 6分 / Questions 8分 / Proposal Plan 6分 / Writing 12分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 2分 ＝ Core 60分

Service Note
A town operates three sharing points where residents and local stores can leave clean, usable household items such as stationery, small storage boxes, and unopened daily-use goods. Other residents may collect the items during opening hours.

After six months, the program receives many items, yet some remain uncollected and must be transferred out because storage becomes crowded. Staff first considered asking for more contributions. A review suggested that the main problem was not total supply but a mismatch between delivery timing, storage space, and when residents could visit.

Rules
• Items must be clean and ready for immediate reuse.
• Large furniture and electrical appliances are not accepted.
• Items cannot be reserved for more than two hours.
• When storage is full, new contributions may be redirected to another site.
• Uncollected items may be transferred to a different site at the end of the week.

Table
Site | Items received/day | Collected locally | Storage spaces | Closing time
Station | 72 | 78% | 24 | 21:00
Library | 48 | 91% | 20 | 19:00
Community Center | 55 | 69% | 14 | 18:00

User Comments
A: “The station is easy to reach after work, but popular items disappear quickly.”
B: “The community center often has useful items left, but I cannot get there before it closes.”
C: “The library receives fewer items, but most are collected on the same day.”

Questions
Q1｜RQ13
Name two different mismatches between supply, storage, and resident access that are visible across the sources.

Q2｜D03
Why would asking for more contributions be an incomplete first response?

Q3｜RQ13
Which site shows the clearest access-time problem?

Proposal Writing
In 100–120 English words, recommend one priority change. Use at least two sources and explain one limitation or trade-off.

Self-check
□ I separated supply quantity from timing and access.
□ I used collection rate as well as contribution volume.
□ I considered storage constraints.
□ I included one limitation or trade-off.

解答・解説・自己修正

Q1｜解答例
Any two of the following. Time: useful items remain at the Community Center, which closes at 18:00, when some residents cannot visit (Comment B). Storage: the Community Center has only 14 storage spaces for 55 items a day, and only 69% are collected locally. Demand by site: popular items disappear quickly at the Station (Comment A) and most Library items are collected the same day (91%), so demand and stock differ across sites.

Q2｜解答例
Because total contributions are already substantial, while uncollected items are linked to closing times, storage limits, and the timing of resident access.

Q3｜解答例
The Community Center, because it has the lowest local collection rate, the earliest closing time, and a user comment describing difficulty arriving before closure.

Model writing
The town should first extend the Community Center’s pickup window on selected days rather than seek more contributions. The site already receives many items, but only 69% are collected locally, and User B reports that the 18:00 closing time makes access difficult after work. A later pickup period could match existing supply with resident availability and reduce the need to transfer items elsewhere. However, longer hours would require additional staffing, so the town should test the change on high-volume days and compare collection rates before expanding it permanently. That comparison would show whether longer hours improve access enough to justify the staffing cost.

## 解説の考え方

### この問題で考えること
「物が余る＝提供量を増やせばよい」と反応せず、**どこで供給と利用が噛み合っていないか**を特定してから改善案を選ぶ。

### 考え方
資料は total supply shortage より **time / access / storage mismatch** を示している。Community Center は 55 items/day を受け取る一方で local collection は 69%、closing は18:00で、User B も仕事後に間に合わないと述べる。ここから、priority change は「物を増やす」より「既存の物にアクセスできる時間を合わせる」方が直接原因に対応していると判断できる。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「集まらないなら寄付を増やす」  
○ 「供給は既にあるが、受取可能時間と利用者の生活時間がずれているので、特定日の受取時間を延ばす」  
改善点は、**observed problem → bottleneck → intervention** を一致させていること。

### 答案の組み立て
- **Position**: Community Center の受取時間を一部の日で延長する
- **Reason**: supply ではなく access-time mismatch が主要な詰まり
- **Mechanism**: 来訪可能時間が広がる → 既存在庫が受け取られる → transfer / storage pressure が減る
- **Support**: 69% collection、18:00 closing、User B
- **Qualification**: staffing cost が増える
- **Return**: high-volume days で試行し collection rate を比較してから拡大

### 模範解答の読み方
Model writing は、提案→資料根拠→mechanism→staffing という反対要素→小規模テスト、の順で進む。最後の test は新しい理由ではなく、qualification を処理して提案を実行可能にする役割を持つ。

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
改善提案では、**量不足 / 配分ミス / 時間ミス / 容量制約**を分ける。問題と同じ場所に intervention を当てると、提案が具体的になる。
