E104｜科学政策の高密度英文：並列・挿入・名詞化をほどいて和訳する

P3 / LT02 / A28・A29 / RF04 / TF12



今日の狙い

高密度な学術英文で、並列範囲・省略・挿入・名詞化を同時に保持する。難語を個別に訳すより先に、圧縮された構造を節へ展開し、日本語として自然な関係へ戻す。



時間配分

Dense Sentence Guide 6分 / Reading 16分 / Questions 10分 / Japanese Translation 14分 / Structure Review 6分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 5分 ＝ Core 60分



Dense Sentence Guide

1. finite verbを先に取る

2. and / or が何と何を並べるか決める

3. dash・comma・parenthesisの挿入を一度外す

4. 名詞化を「誰が／何が〜する」の節へ戻す

5. 最後に日本語の語順へ組み直す



Reading

[1] Scientific advice is often described as if the main task were to produce a more accurate prediction. In public policy, however, the difficulty is not only the uncertainty of the prediction itself but also the need to decide which uncertainties matter for action, which can be reduced through additional evidence, and which must instead be managed through decisions that remain revisable.



[2] This distinction matters because the demand for “more evidence,” reasonable as it may sound, can conceal several different requests: a request for narrower numerical estimates, for better identification of causal mechanisms, or for evidence that a policy will remain acceptable under conditions not represented in the original study. These requests are related, but satisfying one does not automatically satisfy the others.



[3] A transport model, for example, may predict average travel-time changes with considerable precision while offering much weaker evidence about how benefits and burdens are distributed across neighborhoods. Improving the model’s overall predictive accuracy would not, by itself, answer the distributional question. Nor would the discovery of a plausible mechanism guarantee that the estimated effect would remain stable after people changed their behavior in response to the policy.



[4] Policy decisions therefore depend not only on the reduction of uncertainty but also on its classification. Uncertainty about a minor parameter may be tolerable, whereas uncertainty about whether a policy systematically disadvantages a particular group may justify a pilot, an additional safeguard, or a delay. The relevant question is not whether uncertainty can be eliminated, but whether the remaining uncertainty has been made visible and connected to an appropriate decision rule.

Reading answers

Q1

1. narrower numerical estimates

2. better identification of causal mechanisms

3. evidence that a policy will remain acceptable under conditions not represented in the original study



Q2｜解答例

whether / how uncertainty is reduced



Q3｜解答例

Policy decisions require not merely reducing uncertainty but identifying what kind of uncertainty remains and linking it to an appropriate response.



A28/A29｜高密度構造

A28：表面上近い語を並列と決めず、共通する導入部を探す。

A29：reduction / identification / discovery などの名詞化を動詞中心の節へ戻すと論理関係が見えやすい。



RF04｜英文和訳

Model translation

(1) しかし公共政策では、難しさは予測そのものに不確実性があることだけではなく、どの不確実性が行動を決める上で重要なのか、どれが追加の証拠によって小さくできるのか、そしてどれはむしろ後から見直せる決定によって管理すべきなのかを判断しなければならない点にもある。



(2) 重要なのは、不確実性をなくせるかどうかではなく、残る不確実性が見える形にされ、それに適した意思決定の規則と結び付けられているかどうかである。
