Reading
Not every important message is the same kind of communication. A local government may inform residents about a new recycling rule, notify them of a planned road closure, or alert them to an immediate flood risk. The content and urgency of the message affect which verb is most natural.


Inform is broad. It focuses on giving someone information they need. Notify is more formal and is often used for official decisions, scheduled events, or required notices. Alert adds the idea of directing attention to a danger or urgent problem.


The distinction matters in emergencies. A city may notify residents that an evacuation center will open at 6 p.m. and alert people in a riverside area to rapidly rising water. Both messages are important, but the second one is specifically designed to draw attention to danger. The same event can therefore require several different messages. Residents may first be informed about general storm preparation, later notified of a school closure, and finally alerted to immediate flooding if conditions worsen. Choosing the verb helps clarify what the message is trying to do.


Good notification also requires a clear recipient. A message may be technically accurate but ineffective if it does not reach the people who need it. Authorities therefore need systems that can identify who should be informed, notified, or alerted and through which channel. Delivery method matters as well. A formal email may be appropriate for a scheduled change, while a rapidly developing hazard may require text alerts, sirens, radio, or repeated messages through several channels. The verb choice therefore reflects not only content but also the communicative function.

解説補強｜「知らせる」の目的を先に決める

### この問題で考えること
inform / notify / alert を同義語として選ぶのではなく、**messageが相手に何をさせるためのものか**を先に決めます。

### 処理手順
- background / practical information → inform
- official scheduled decision / change → notify
- immediate danger requiring attention → alert

### 弱い考え方との比較
× alert residents about every possible risk
○ alert residents when evidence indicates an urgent threat requiring action

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
語彙選択では「日本語訳」より **recipient + message function + urgency** を見ると、別題材でも同じ判断ができます。
