Plan
Fast Introduce
- Main point：各段落のfunctionを並べ、具体例を落として全文に共通する中心命題を残す
- Output switch：英語の意味関係を確定してから、日本語として自然な順序へ組み直す
- Parallel / contrast：but / and の左右を一度そろえてから解釈・和訳する


Text A
[1] A public library notices that some residents rarely use its digital services even though the services are free. At first, staff assume that the problem is lack of interest. Interviews reveal a different pattern. Some older residents do not know how to create accounts, some users rely on phones with small screens, and others are unsure which online source is trustworthy. The formal service is available to everyone, but practical access differs.


[2] The library responds by offering short support sessions, simplifying account instructions, and adding clearer labels to its website. These changes do not guarantee that everyone will use the service. They reduce avoidable barriers that prevented some residents from making an informed choice about whether the service was useful to them.


[3] The broader lesson is that access should not be measured only by whether a service exists. Institutions also need to ask whether people can realistically understand and use it. This does not require identical support for every person; it requires attention to barriers that systematically prevent participation.


Text B
A report on the program describes “the gradual increase in successful account creation observed among first-time users who attended the short support sessions.” The report notes that the increase does not prove that the sessions alone caused every improvement. Website changes occurred at the same time, and users who chose to attend the sessions may already have been more motivated. The finding is therefore useful but limited: it suggests that the combined changes may have helped, while leaving uncertainty about the contribution of each component.

Q2｜B

Q3｜解答
head noun = increase
relevant users = first-time users who attended the short support sessions
observed among ... はincreaseがどこで観察されたかを示す。


Q4｜解答
useful / limited
役立つ情報ではあるが、因果寄与を一つに特定できないという評価をbutで対比している。


Q5｜解答例
同時期にウェブサイトも変更され、参加者にも元々の意欲の差があり得るため、改善を支援セッションだけの効果とは特定できないから。


Q6｜和訳例
「形式上は誰でもそのサービスを利用できるが、実際に利用できる度合いには差がある。」


Japanese Output｜解答例
「制度やサービスが形式上すべての人に開かれているだけでなく、利用を妨げる回避可能な障壁を減らし、実質的に理解・利用できる機会を確保することが重要である。」
