E055｜障害のある人のスポーツ参加：同じ扱いは本当に公平か
P2 / LT01 / A05 段落機能 / RQ07 / TF04


今日の狙い
段落の「内容」だけでなく、「文章全体の中で何をしている段落か」を取る。各段落を1文で要約するだけで終わらず、前後との関係を見て function verb で説明する。


時間配分
Strategy 5分 / Reading 18分 / Paragraph Function Check 10分 / Questions 10分 / Function Output 7分 / Self-check 4分 / Post-solve 6分 ＝ Core 60分


Paragraph Function Guide
内容要約：その段落は何を言っているか
段落機能：その内容を使って、文章全体をどう前へ進めているか
今回は次の4語を使う
introduces = 問題・問い・論点を導入する
explains = 原因・仕組み・理由を説明する
qualifies = 直前の主張を否定せず、条件や限界を加える
generalizes = 個別の議論から、より広い原則へ広げる


Reading support
barrier = 参加や利用を妨げる障壁
accommodation = 障壁を減らすための調整・配慮
effective opportunity = 形式上ではなく、実際に利用・参加できる機会


Reading
[1] A city opens a new community sports center and announces that every resident will be treated in exactly the same way. The same membership fee applies to everyone, the same registration form is used, and the same schedule is published online. At first, this policy appears fair because no group receives special treatment. Yet equal rules do not always produce equal access. A person who uses a wheelchair may face a staircase at the entrance. Someone with a visual impairment may be unable to use an online form that has not been designed for screen-reading software. The rule is identical, but the practical opportunity to participate is not.


[2] This difference matters because barriers are often created not by a person's condition alone but by the way an environment is designed. If a sports class assumes that every participant can hear verbal instructions, stand for long periods, or move quickly between rooms, some people may be excluded before the activity even begins. Removing such barriers can require changes: a ramp, clearer signs, flexible instructions, different equipment, or additional staff support. These changes are sometimes described as giving certain people an advantage. A better question, however, is whether the original arrangement already favored people whose needs matched the standard design. What looks neutral on paper may therefore have unequal effects in practice.


[3] This does not mean that every difference in treatment is automatically fair. Resources are limited, and accommodations should be connected to actual barriers. A city cannot promise every possible form of support in every situation. It can, however, ask what prevents participation, how serious the barrier is, and what change would remove it without creating unreasonable costs or safety risks. In this sense, fairness is not the simple opposite of efficiency. Good design can sometimes improve both. Clearer signs help visitors who do not know the building. Flexible instructions can help beginners as well as people with disabilities. A change introduced for one group may therefore make the service easier to use for many others.


[4] The broader lesson is that participation should be judged by effective opportunity, not only by formally equal rules. When institutions focus only on whether everyone receives the same treatment, they may overlook differences in what people are actually able to do. When they focus on barriers and outcomes, they can make more precise decisions about support. The goal is not to guarantee identical results for everyone. It is to make sure that avoidable features of the system do not block people from taking part before their own choices and abilities have a chance to matter.

Paragraph Function Check
1. 1-B / 2-D / 3-A / 4-C
Paragraph 1は「全員を同じに扱う」という一見公平なルールを置き、その直後にequal accessとのズレを提示するので introduces。
Paragraph 2は、そのズレがなぜ起こるかをenvironmental designの観点から説明し、「配慮＝特別扱い」という見方を問い直すので explains。
Paragraph 3は、配慮なら何でも正当化されるわけではないと条件・限界を加えるので qualifies。
Paragraph 4は、スポーツ施設の具体例を超えてeffective opportunityという一般原則へ広げるので generalizes。


2. “This does not mean that ...”
この表現は直前の主張そのものを否定せず、「そこからここまでは言えない」と射程を狭める典型的なqualification signal。


Reading answers
Q1｜C
Paragraph 3で、clearer signsは建物に不慣れな来訪者にも、flexible instructionsは初心者にも役立つと述べている。特定集団向けの変更が他の利用者にも便益を持つことが本文根拠。


Q2｜B
Paragraph 2の中心は「barrierは個人の状態だけでなく環境設計から生まれうる」という仕組みの説明。後半では“special treatment”という見方を、standard designそのものが一部の人に有利だった可能性から捉え直している。


Q3｜A
neutral on paper = 形式上は中立・同一、unequal effects in practice = 実際の影響は集団によって異なりうる、という対比。


Q4｜解答例
Identical rules can fail to create equal access because people may face different barriers in the same environment. A rule can be formally equal while the practical opportunity to participate is unequal.


A05｜段落機能
「内容要約」と「段落機能」を混ぜない。
内容要約なら、Paragraph 3 = “Resources and accommodations have limits.”
段落機能なら、Paragraph 3 = “It qualifies the previous claim by setting limits on accommodations.”
後者には、直前の段落との関係が入っている。P2以降は「何が書いてあるか」だけでなく、「なぜここにこの段落が必要か」を読む。


RQ07｜段落機能問題
解く順番は3段階。
1. その段落を1文で要約する
2. 前の段落から何が変わったかを見る
3. introduces / explains / qualifies / contrasts / exemplifies / generalizes などのfunction verbで言い換える
本文中の具体例だけを選択肢に見つけても、それが段落全体の機能とは限らない。


Function Output
解答例：
Paragraph 3 qualifies the argument by setting limits on accommodations and explaining that support must be connected to actual barriers, costs, and safety.
