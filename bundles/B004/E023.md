Reading
A disaster warning has to do more than announce that danger exists. It should help people understand what is known, what remains uncertain, and what action they should take. A message that communicates only the danger but not the recommended action may create fear without helping people respond.


Uncertainty is difficult to convey. Authorities may worry that admitting uncertainty will make a warning sound weak. However, pretending to know more than the evidence supports can create false reassurance or damage trust when conditions change.


Timing and language also matter. A technically accurate message is not useful if it arrives too late or if residents cannot understand it. Multilingual communities may use automatic translation to communicate information quickly, but high-stakes warnings often benefit from human review because a small change in meaning can affect behavior. Accessibility matters as well. A warning delivered only through a smartphone app may not reach people without reliable internet, while an audio warning may be difficult for someone with hearing loss. Using several channels can reduce the chance that one communication barrier prevents timely action, especially for vulnerable residents during rapidly changing conditions.


The tone of a warning should match the situation. Officials may express serious concern without claiming that the worst outcome is certain. They can convey urgency by giving clear deadlines, locations, and actions rather than by using dramatic language alone.


Effective risk communication therefore clearly separates current facts, uncertainty, and action. The goal is not to remove all uncertainty from the message, but to communicate enough information for people to make safer decisions.

解説補強｜risk communicationはcertaintyとactionを分ける

### この問題で考えること
不確実だから何も言えないのではなく、**known / unknown / action** を分けて伝えることが核心です。

### 考え方
uncertain evidence
→ certaintyを偽って上げない
→ ただし current risk / possible change / recommended action は明確にする
→ trustを保ちつつ行動可能なmessageにする

### 弱い考え方との比較
× We are not sure, so residents should just be careful.
○ Authorities should state what is known, what may change, and what residents should do now.

### 模範解答の読み方
communicate はinformation、convey はurgency・meaning、express はspeaker stanceです。どの情報機能を担うかで動詞を選んでいます。

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
risk messageは **evidence status → uncertainty boundary → concrete action** の順に作ります。
