Quick Guide
finite verb = tense / modalを持つ動詞
A clause normally has its own subject + finite verb.


Examples
Species decline when habitats disappear.
finite verbs: decline / disappear


Protecting wetlands can reduce habitat loss.
finite verb: can reduce
Protecting = non-finite


When a clause begins with although / because / if / when, do not mistake that clause for the whole sentence.
First locate every finite verb. Then decide which clause is the main clause.

Reading
Biodiversity refers not only to the number of species in an area but also to the variety of genes, habitats, and ecological relationships that allow ecosystems to function. Although public attention often focuses on rare animals, common plants, insects, and microorganisms can also play essential roles.


When habitats are divided by roads, farms, or urban development, populations that once exchanged individuals may become isolated. If a local population becomes too small, it can be more vulnerable to disease, environmental change, or the loss of genetic diversity. Protecting one small site may therefore be insufficient when the species that use it also depend on nearby areas.


One response is to create habitat corridors. A corridor does not need to be a completely untouched strip of land. It can include riverbanks, hedges, small forests, or other spaces that allow organisms to move between larger habitats. If these connections are designed carefully, they may support migration and help populations recover after local disturbance. Their value also depends on quality. A narrow strip that contains food, shelter, and suitable vegetation may help more than a larger area that organisms cannot safely cross. Monitoring is therefore needed to determine whether species actually use the connection.


However, conservation can create conflicts. Farmers may worry that new restrictions will reduce productive land, while local governments may face pressure to build housing or transport infrastructure. A policy that protects biodiversity without considering these costs may be difficult to maintain.


For this reason, conservation plans often combine protected areas with restoration, incentives, and monitoring. The aim is not to freeze every landscape in its current form. It is to preserve enough ecological function that species and habitats can continue to adapt as conditions change.

### 再利用する処理手順
この回の中心はbiodiversityの知識ではなく、**意味を細かく取る前にclauseの骨格を確保すること**です。

1. tense / modalを持つfinite verbを全て拾う
2. 各finite verbのsubjectを対応させる
3. although / because / if / when / relative clause の境界を置く
4. main clauseを一文で言い直す
5. その後にmodifierを戻す

### 弱い処理との比較
× 左から順に全単語を訳し、途中で主語を見失う
○ main clauseを先に固定し、subordinate informationを後から足す

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
unknown wordがあっても、**finite verbとmain clauseが取れれば文の中心は保持できる**という順序を固定します。
