Reading
Students often judge learning by how familiar material feels during study. Rereading a page several times can make the sentences easier to recognize, but this familiarity does not guarantee that the same information can be recalled later without support.


Retrieval practice changes the task. Instead of looking again at an answer, the learner tries to produce it from memory. The attempt may be incomplete, yet the effort reveals what can and cannot be recalled. Feedback then helps correct errors before they become stable.


Spacing can make retrieval harder in the short term. When some time has passed, an answer is less immediately available, so recalling it requires more effort. However, practice that is always easy may give an unrealistic impression of mastery. The goal is not to maximize difficulty but to create enough distance that memory has to be used.


The same principle applies to language learning. A learner who recognizes contribute to in a passage may still fail to produce it when writing about a new topic. To strengthen retrieval, the prompt should provide the meaning and context without displaying the exact expression every time.


Good retrieval therefore reduces support gradually. Early practice may include choices or partial cues. Later practice should require the learner to select and produce an expression without seeing the original list. Immediate, specific feedback remains especially important because repeated retrieval of an incorrect form can gradually strengthen the wrong pattern over time. Success means not merely “I know this when I see it” but “I can retrieve this accurately when the context requires it.”

Rereading can make information feel familiar because the answer remains visible. However, that feeling does not show whether a learner can recall the same information without support.


別解
Repeated exposure may lead to strong recognition while contributing less to independent recall. Retrieval practice tests whether the information can actually be produced when the original text is absent.

### 再利用する処理手順
「見れば分かる」はwritingで使えることを保証しません。必要なのは、**意味・文脈から表現を自力で取り出すこと**です。
