E167｜公共施設の予約枠：混合資料から改善案を書く

予約枠不足を単純な「需要過多」と決めつけず、無断キャンセル・時間帯偏在・解放ルールを資料から特定し、改善案を書く。

Service Note
A public community center allows residents to reserve meeting rooms online. Demand is highest on weekday evenings. Staff have received complaints that popular time slots appear fully booked even though some rooms later remain unused.
A review found that the problem was not only high demand. Some users made reservations several weeks in advance and later failed to cancel them. In addition, cancelled rooms were sometimes returned to the system too late for other users to notice and make plans.
Rules
• Reservations may be made up to 30 days in advance.
• Users may hold up to three future reservations.
• Cancellation is allowed until the reservation start time.
• Repeated no-shows may lead to temporary booking restrictions.
• Same-day released rooms appear online immediately.
Table
Time slot | Booking rate | No-show rate | Average time of cancellation before use
09:00–12:00 | 62% | 5% | 18 h
13:00–17:00 | 71% | 7% | 11 h
18:00–20:00 | 96% | 16% | 3 h
20:00–22:00 | 91% | 13% | 4 h
User Comments
A: “Evening rooms are almost always shown as full, so I stopped checking.”
B: “I sometimes cancel after work when I realize my group cannot meet.”
C: “A room appeared at 18:10, but by then I had already changed our plans.”
Questions
Q1｜RQ13
What operational problem is supported by the note, table, and comments?
Q2｜D03
Why would simply adding more rooms be an incomplete first response?
Q3｜RQ13
Which time slots show the clearest combination of high demand and inefficient reservation use?
Proposal Writing
In 100–120 English words, recommend one priority change to the reservation system. Use at least two sources and explain one trade-off or limitation.
Self-check

□ I used cancellation timing as evidence.
□ I did not assume every booking should be restricted.

解答・解説・自己修正
Q1｜解答例
Popular evening slots combine very high booking rates with more no-shows and late cancellations, so apparent full capacity does not always become actual room use.
Q2｜解答例
Because some existing capacity is lost through no-shows and late release. Expanding rooms would cost more without first correcting avoidable inefficiency.
Q3｜解答例
18:00–20:00 and 20:00–22:00.
Model writing
The center should require users to reconfirm popular evening reservations several hours before use. The table shows that evening slots have both very high booking rates and relatively high no-show rates, while cancellations often occur only three to four hours before the reservation. User Comment C also suggests that very late releases are difficult for others to use. A confirmation rule could return unwanted rooms to the system earlier. However, it may inconvenience users whose plans change suddenly, so the rule should apply first only to high-demand evening slots and be tested before wider use. This would show whether earlier releases actually improve room reuse.
## 解説の考え方

### この問題で考えること
「fully booked」を実需要そのものとみなさず、**予約されたcapacityが実利用へ変換される途中でどこが失われるか**を見る。

### 考え方
evening slots はbooking rateが高いだけでなく no-show rate も高く、cancellationも直前。つまり demand は高いが、reserved capacityの一部が late release によって他利用者へ再配分されない。reconfirmation はこの **reservation → actual use** の変換ロスを減らす intervention である。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「満室表示が多い → 部屋を増設」  
○ 「高需要に加えてno-show / late cancellationで既存capacityが失われているため、まずrelease timingを改善する」

### 答案の組み立て
- **Position**: high-demand evening slotsにreconfirmation
- **Reason**: avoidable unused capacity がある
- **Mechanism**: 不要予約を早くrelease → 他利用者が計画・再予約できる
- **Support**: evening booking/no-show/cancellation data + Comment C
- **Qualification**: sudden plan changesへの負担
- **Return**: 限定pilotでreuse改善を検証

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
capacity shortageでは、**物理capacity不足**と**既存capacityの利用ロス**を分ける。


Self-correction
