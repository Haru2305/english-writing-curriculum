E166｜給水制限の案内：掲示・FAQ・表を日本語で統合する
P3 / LT03 / RQ13 / RF06 / D10 / SS05 / TF17
今日の狙い
掲示・FAQ・使用量表を統合し、給水制限を「禁止事項の一覧」ではなく、目的・対象・例外・時間帯の構造で日本語要約する。
時間配分
Silent Plan 1分 / Notice 8分 / FAQ 6分 / Table 8分 / Questions 10分 / Integration Plan 5分 / Japanese Summary 14分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 5分 ＝ Core 60分
Notice｜Temporary Water Restrictions
Because reservoir levels are unusually low, the city will limit selected nonessential outdoor water use for four weeks.
Restricted uses:
• lawn watering
• washing private vehicles at home
• filling decorative pools
Allowed uses:
• drinking, cooking, bathing, and sanitation
• medically necessary use
• watering newly planted trees during the first two weeks after planting
• commercial uses that meet separate efficiency rules
Outdoor watering is permitted only from 19:00 to 22:00 on assigned days.
FAQ
Q: Is all outdoor water use prohibited?
A: No. Some uses remain allowed, and newly planted trees have a temporary exception.
Q: Why are evening hours used?
A: Lower temperatures reduce evaporation compared with midday watering.
Q: Does every household receive the same assigned day?
A: No. Days are divided by neighborhood to spread demand across the week.
Q: Will the restrictions end automatically after four weeks?
A: The city will review reservoir conditions before deciding whether to end, extend, or modify them.
Table
Use category | Share of household water before restriction | Expected reduction
Indoor essential | 58% | 2%
Lawn watering | 18% | 45%
Vehicle washing | 7% | 60%
Other outdoor | 9% | 25%
Other indoor | 8% | 5%
Questions
Q1｜RQ13
Why are lawn watering and vehicle washing central targets of the restriction?
Q2｜D10
What exception limits a simple rule that “outdoor watering is restricted”?
Q3｜RQ09
Using the table, roughly how much might total household water use fall, and why is this not guaranteed?
Q4｜RQ13
Why are assigned days and evening hours both part of the policy?
Japanese Summary
資料全体を150〜180字の日本語で要約する。
「目的」「主な対象」「時間帯」「例外」「見直し条件」を含める。

解答・解説・自己修正
Q1｜解答例
They account for meaningful shares of household use and are expected to produce relatively large reductions without restricting essential indoor needs.
別解：Notice が基準とする「nonessential outdoor use（非必須の屋外利用）」にあたるため、も可。
Q2｜解答例
Newly planted trees may still be watered during their first two weeks, even though outdoor watering is generally restricted.
別解：commercial uses that meet separate efficiency rules、また指定日の 19:00–22:00 には屋外散水が許可されること、も例外として可。
Q3｜解答例
The policy is likely to reduce total household use. Weighting each expected reduction by its share gives roughly a 16% fall (for example, lawn watering 18% × 45% ≈ 8 points and vehicle washing 7% × 60% ≈ 4 points), but this is an expectation that depends on actual compliance, not a guaranteed result.
Q4｜解答例
Evening hours reduce evaporation, while assigned days spread demand across the week so that many households do not use water at the same time.
Model Japanese summary
貯水量が低下したため、市は4週間、芝生散水や自家用車洗浄など非必須の屋外利用を中心に制限する。この二つは家庭の水使用の約4分の1を占め、削減の見込みも大きい。屋外散水は指定日の19～22時に限られ、植栽直後の樹木などには例外がある。生活に必要な用途は維持し、期間終了時には貯水状況を再評価して、終了・延長・変更を判断する。
Self-correction
restriction / exception / timing / review の四層を残す。禁止事項だけを並べない。
