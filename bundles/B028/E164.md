E164｜都市の浸水対策：高密度文と表から容量・頻度・限界を読む

高密度文をほどき、雨水貯留設備の「容量・降雨頻度・超過時の挙動」を本文と表から統合する。
時間配分

Reading
[1] A city installs underground tanks that temporarily store stormwater during heavy rain. The tanks do not remove water from the system permanently. They delay part of the flow so that drainage pipes receive less water during the most intense period.
[2] This makes storage capacity important, but capacity alone does not determine performance. A tank large enough for a common storm may fill during a rarer extreme event. The same tank may also perform differently depending on how much water it contains before rain begins.
Dense Sentence
What determines whether temporary storage meaningfully reduces flood pressure is not simply the nominal volume of the tank but the relationship among available empty capacity at the start of the event, the rate at which runoff enters the system, the duration of high inflow, and the speed with which stored water can later be released without shifting the problem downstream.
[3] The city compares several simulated storms.
Table
Event | Rainfall pattern | Tank filled | Peak flow reduction | Overflow
A | Short, moderate | 62% | 31% | None
B | Long, moderate | 96% | 18% | Small
C | Short, intense | 100% | 22% | Moderate
D | Long, intense | 100% early | 9% | Large
[4] The table shows why a tank can still help even when it eventually fills. During Event C, storage reduces the early peak before capacity is exhausted. By contrast, in Event D the tank fills early and provides little protection during the later part of the storm.
[5] This does not mean that bigger tanks are always the best investment. Additional storage can be costly, and some locations may benefit more from reducing runoff, improving drainage routes, or operating several smaller storage sites together.
[6] The useful question is therefore not “Does the tank prevent flooding?” but “Under which rainfall and starting conditions does it reduce peak pressure enough to matter?” That framing treats storage as one component of a system rather than as a guarantee.

解答・解説・自己修正
Q1｜解答例
Tank volume alone does not determine flood reduction. Performance depends on how much empty capacity is available before rain. It also depends on inflow rate and duration. Finally, stored water must be released later without creating another downstream problem.
Q2｜和訳例
一時貯留が浸水圧力をどれだけ有意に減らせるかを決めるのはタンクの名目容量だけではなく、降雨開始時の空き容量、雨水が流入する速さ、高流入が続く時間、そして下流へ問題を移さずに貯留水を後で放流できる速さの関係である。
Q3｜解答
Event C.
Q4｜解答例
Event D combines high intensity with long duration, so the tank fills early and cannot continue absorbing later inflow. Event C is intense but short enough for storage to reduce the early peak substantially.
Q5｜解答例
More capacity may improve performance in some storms, but its value depends on cost, rainfall patterns, starting conditions, and alternative ways to manage runoff.

## 解説の考え方

### この問題で考えること
tank の性能を「何m³あるか」だけで評価せず、**available capacity × inflow rate × duration × release** の時間的な関係で考える。

### 考え方
temporary storage の役割は水を消すことではなく、peak flow を時間的にずらすこと。Event C は最終的に満水でも、その前にearly peakを吸収するので便益がある。Event D はhigh inflowが長く続き早期に満水になるため、後半のpeakを吸収できない。したがって「満水になったか」だけでは性能を判断できない。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「100% filled = 効果なし」  
× 「大きいtankほど常に良い」  
○ 「どの時点で容量を使い切り、その前後でpeakをどれだけ削れたか」を見る

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
buffer / reserve / storage は、**総量より、必要な瞬間に余力が残っているか**が重要。病床、在庫、電力蓄電、交通余裕時間にも転用できる。
