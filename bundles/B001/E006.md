Text A｜Community clinics
A town may build a new community clinic to improve access to basic care. The new building can reduce travel for some residents, but its effect depends on staffing and opening hours. If the clinic cannot retain enough professionals, patients may still travel to a larger hospital for many services. Transport also matters because some patients cannot easily reach the clinic even when it is geographically closer. Local officials might extend opening hours, but this can increase pressure on a small staff unless recruitment or scheduling changes at the same time. A clinic can therefore improve one part of access without removing every regional barrier. The relevant question is not simply whether a facility exists, but whether people can reach it, whether appropriate services are available, and whether the workforce can support those services over time.


Text B｜After-school learning
An after-school study program is offered free of charge, but participation remains uneven. Some students can stay after school, while others must travel long distances or help at home. The program may contribute to better learning opportunities, yet availability alone does not guarantee equal use. The school first assumes that cost is the main barrier, but teachers notice that several students leave early because the last bus to their area departs soon after classes end. Others share devices with family members and cannot use the online materials at home. One teacher suggests that the school also provide a quiet study space before classes begin. Another suggests offering recorded explanations that students can review at different times. The teachers also notice that students who miss one session often hesitate to return because they are unsure what material they have missed. A short weekly summary could reduce that problem without changing the entire program. These changes may help different groups, but neither one is likely to remove every disadvantage on its own.


Text C｜Care planning
Two districts have a similar proportion of older residents, but they differ in workforce availability and transport. In one district, demand for home care has risen quickly because more people live far from major facilities. In the other, services are easier to reach, but many care workers are approaching retirement. Local officials plan to raise training capacity, but new workers will not become available immediately. They are also considering transport support and better coordination with family caregivers. The same age statistics can therefore lead to different practical problems. One district may need to reduce travel barriers, while the other may need to retain workers and reorganize existing services. The districts may share a long-term goal, but they can still require different short-term responses because the conditions surrounding care are not the same. Similar statistics do not always imply identical policy priorities in real planning situations.

Building a clinic may contribute to better access, but it does not solve every regional problem. Rural communities can differ from cities in staffing and transport. I suggest that policy makers improve staffing support as well as physical facilities.

解説補強｜Checkpointで残す思考回路

### このCheckpointで考えること
Bundle 1の共通点は、語法そのものではなく、**一つの要因・一つの数字・一つの対策だけで複雑な結果を決めない**ことです。

3つのTextはすべて、
visible solution / visible statistic  
→ 実際の結果までに複数の条件がある  
→ 一つの対策はその一部だけを動かす  
→ 他のbarrierが残れば結果は限定的  
という同じ構造を持っています。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× clinicができた → access problem solved  
× free programがある → equal opportunity achieved  
× older populationが同じ → same care policy

○ facilityがあってもstaffing・transportが必要  
○ programがあってもtime・device・return pathwayが必要  
○ age statisticが同じでもworkforce・transportが違う

改善点は、**存在・導入・平均値を、そのまま最終結果と同一視しないこと**です。

### Short Writingの組み立て
まず選んだTextについて、
1. one solution can help
2. but one or more conditions remain
3. therefore the same solution may not be enough
の3段階を作ります。

その後で、意味関係に合う表現を選びます。
- 比較対象／相違点 → differ from / differ in
- 因果の強さ → cause / lead to / result in / contribute to
- 提案の行為者 → suggest doing / suggest that S V
- 変化の向き → rise / raise

つまり、**表現を先に詰め込むのではなく、論理関係を決めてから語法を選ぶ**のがCheckpointの到達点です。

### 模範解答の読み方
モデル答案は、最初に「一つの対策は役立つ」と認め、その後にremaining barriersを置き、最後に追加策または条件へ戻しています。  
これは **Reason → Mechanism → Qualification → Decision** の短い形です。

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
新しい題材でも、まず
**何が変わるか／何が残るか／その結果どこまで言えるか**
を整理します。  
語法は、その論理関係を正確に英語化するために使います。
