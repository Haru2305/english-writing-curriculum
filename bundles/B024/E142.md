E142｜公共施設の案内：掲示・FAQ・メールから改善点を推論する

P3 / LT03 / SS04 / RQ13 / RQ09 / RF03 / TF11



今日の狙い

掲示・FAQ・利用者メールをまたいで、明示されていない運用上の問題を推論する。資料ごとに読むのではなく、同じ利用行動に関する情報を統合する。



時間配分

Source Map 4分 / Notice 7分 / FAQ 7分 / Emails 8分 / Questions 10分 / Japanese Explanation 10分 / Improvement Task 6分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 5分 ＝ Core 60分



Notice｜Community Study Hall

Opening hours: 9:00–20:00

Quiet rooms can be reserved for up to 90 minutes.

Same-day reservations are available at the front desk.

Reservations are cancelled if the user has not arrived 10 minutes after the start time.

Food is allowed only in the lounge.



FAQ

Q: Can I reserve a room online?

A: Online reservation is available only to registered users. Registration must be completed at the front desk with identification.



Q: Can someone else use my room if I am late?

A: After 10 minutes, the reservation is automatically cancelled and the room becomes available to another user.



Q: Can I extend my reservation?

A: Yes, but only if nobody has reserved the following time slot.



Email 1

“I wanted to use a quiet room after work. I checked the website from home, but I could not register online. By the time I arrived, all same-day rooms were booked.”



Email 2

“I had a 17:00 reservation but my bus was delayed. I arrived at 17:12 and the room had already been given to someone else. I understand the rule, but I did not know cancellation was automatic.”



Email 3

“I used the hall several times before learning that registered users could reserve online. The poster near the entrance mentions quiet rooms but does not explain the registration step.”



Questions

Q1｜RQ13

What problem is supported by both the FAQ and Emails 1/3?



Q2｜RQ09

What can reasonably be inferred about users who rely on the poster and notice for booking rules?



Q3｜RQ13

Why is Email 2 not mainly evidence that the 10-minute rule is unfair?



Q4｜RF03

日本語で、利用者が「制度を知る」ことと「制度を使える」ことの違いを70〜100字で説明せよ。



Improvement Task

Propose two changes to the information design, not to the room capacity itself. Explain which source supports each change.



Self-check

□ I connected evidence across sources.

□ I distinguished policy design from information design.

□ I did not infer more than the sources support.

□ My proposed changes address observed barriers.

解答・解説・自己修正



Q1｜解答例

Users must register in person before they can reserve online, but this requirement is not sufficiently visible before arrival.



Q2｜解答例

They may learn key rules (in-person registration, automatic cancellation after 10 minutes) only after arriving, or after losing a room.



Q3｜解答例

The email mainly shows that the automatic cancellation rule was not clearly understood in advance. The user explicitly accepts the rule itself.



Q4｜解答例

制度の存在を知っていても、事前登録や自動取消しなど利用に必要な手順を把握していなければ、希望する時間に予約・利用できないため、認知と利用可能性は同じではない。



Improvement examples

1. Put the in-person registration requirement prominently on the reservation webpage before users attempt to book. Supported by the FAQ and Emails 1/3.

2. Display the automatic 10-minute cancellation rule in the reservation confirmation message. Supported by the FAQ and Email 2.



RQ13｜非連続資料統合

同じ主張を複数資料が支える場合、資料名を列挙せず「どの関係が一致しているか」を答える。



Self-correction

改善案が「部屋を増やす」など資料外の問題へ飛んでいないか確認する。今回はinformation designが対象。
