E143｜地域の食品共有制度：混合資料から提案を書く

P3 / LT03 / SS05 / D03 Review / RQ13 / WF04 / WT03 / TF10



今日の狙い

複数資料から「何が問題か」を特定し、本文の原理を使って実行可能な改善提案を書く。資料の要約だけで終わらず、problem→solutionへ変換する。



時間配分

Mixed-source Plan 4分 / Article 10分 / Rules 6分 / Data 5分 / Questions 8分 / Proposal Plan 7分 / Writing 12分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 5分 ＝ Core 60分



Article

A neighborhood food-sharing program allows stores and households to leave unopened or safely prepared surplus food at three community pickup points. The program was created to reduce waste while making food available to residents who wanted it. During the first two months, donations were high, but a large share of food placed late in the evening remained uncollected until the next morning.



Organizers initially assumed that the main problem was low awareness. They increased online promotion, yet the pattern changed little. Interviews suggested a different issue: many potential users knew about the program but were uncertain about when food would be available and whether a long trip would be worthwhile.



Rules

• Perishable food may be left only between 16:00 and 20:00.

• Uncollected perishable food is removed at 21:00.

• Donors do not need to report what they will bring in advance.

• Pickup points do not guarantee that any particular item will be available.

• Users may take reasonable amounts for personal or household use.



Data

Pickup point | Average evening donations | Average users arriving | Share removed at 21:00

Station | 38 | 31 | 18%

Library | 27 | 14 | 41%

Community Center | 22 | 9 | 48%



User comments

A: “I know the program exists, but I rarely know whether there will be anything left when I arrive.”

B: “The station point is easy for me because I pass it on my way home.”

C: “I would check the library point more often if there were some indication of whether donations had arrived.”



Questions

Q1｜RQ13

What is the main operational problem supported by the article, data, and comments?



Q2｜D03

Why did more promotion fail to solve the problem?



Q3｜RQ13

Which pickup points appear to have the greatest mismatch between donations and user arrival?



Proposal Writing

In 100–120 English words, propose one priority improvement that does not require increasing the amount of donated food. Use at least two sources and explain one limitation of your proposal.



Self-check

□ I identified the problem before proposing a solution.

□ My solution addresses uncertainty or access, not a different problem.

□ I used evidence from more than one source.

□ I included a realistic limitation.

解答・解説・自己修正



Q1｜解答例

The main problem is a mismatch between when/where food is available and whether users know enough to decide that a trip is worthwhile.



Q2｜解答例

Awareness was not the main barrier. Many users already knew about the program but lacked timely information about actual availability.



Q3｜解答例

The library and community center show the largest mismatch, with much higher shares of food remaining uncollected than at the station.



D03｜problem→solution

問題を “low participation” とだけ置くと解決策がぼやける。

problem = uncertainty about availability + location-specific mismatch

まで具体化してからsolutionを書く。



Model writing

The program should first add a simple real-time availability update for each pickup point. Donors or volunteers could mark when food has arrived without listing every item. This would address the uncertainty described in the article and User Comment C, and it may be especially useful at the library and community center, where a large share of donations is removed uncollected. The system should remain simple because detailed item tracking could create extra work and inaccurate information. Even with updates, the program cannot guarantee that food will still be available when a user arrives. The town could then compare pickup reliability before and after implementation.



Self-correction

“advertise more”のように失敗済みの施策を言い換えていないか確認する。資料から問題の機序を取る。



到達目安

A：複数資料から問題を定義し、根拠・限界つき提案へ変換できる。

B：提案は妥当だが、問題定義または根拠が弱い。

C：資料要約か一般論で終わる。
