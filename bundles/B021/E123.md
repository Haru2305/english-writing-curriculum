E123｜家族介護の支援制度：例外を残したまま英語で要約する

P3 / LT03 / D10 Guided / RF05 Guided / TF03



今日の狙い

一般原則と例外条件を読み分け、その限定関係を落とさず英語要約へ圧縮する。短くする過程で “always / everyone” 型に強めない。



時間配分

Guided Integration 5分 / Reading 18分 / Questions 9分 / Qualification Map 7分 / Summary Task 15分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 3分 ＝ Core 60分



Guided Integration

要約前に、

general rule / exception / final qualified rule

を一行ずつ書く。

例外が細部に見えても、主張の適用範囲を変えるなら残す。



Reading

[1] Employers that offer family-care leave often try to define eligibility clearly. A simple rule based on relationship, duration of care, or expected absence can make the program easier to administer and can help workers understand whether they qualify.



[2] Clear rules are valuable, but care situations do not always develop predictably. A worker may suddenly need several days away when a parent is discharged from hospital, while another may need shorter absences spread across many months. A policy designed only for long continuous leave may therefore miss forms of care that are less visible but still substantial.



[3] This does not imply that every family responsibility should automatically qualify. Employers still need boundaries so that the program remains administratively workable and its purpose is understandable. The stronger principle is that eligibility criteria should reflect the kinds of care burden the policy intends to address rather than assume that all serious care has the same schedule.



[4] Flexibility can also create inequity if only some workers can use it. Employees may technically qualify for leave but avoid taking it because they fear lost income, career penalties, or additional workload on colleagues. A policy can therefore be generous on paper while remaining difficult to use in practice.



[5] For this reason, evaluation should include both formal eligibility and actual use. High eligibility with very low uptake may indicate that the policy’s practical conditions remain restrictive. Conversely, high use does not by itself show that all important care needs are covered; some workers may still fall outside the rule.



[6] Effective care-leave policy therefore combines clear criteria with limited flexibility. Rules should be predictable enough to explain, yet broad enough to recognize important patterns of care unless doing so would make the policy impossible to administer consistently.



Questions

Q1｜D10

In the final sentence, which requirement is limited by “unless ...”, and what is the limit?



Q2｜RQ09

Why can a generous policy on paper still be difficult to use?



Summary Task

Summarize the passage in 75–90 English words. Preserve:

- the value of clear criteria

- the reason some flexibility is needed

- one practical barrier to use

- the final qualified principle



Self-check

□ My summary keeps both rule and exception.

□ I did not write that all care situations should qualify.

□ I included actual use, not only formal eligibility.

□ My final sentence is appropriately qualified.

解答・解説・自己修正



Reading answers

Q1｜解答例

The requirement that rules be broad enough to recognize important patterns of care (flexibility) is limited by whether this would make consistent administration impossible. “Predictable enough to explain” is the base rule that stays in place.



Q2｜解答例

Workers may qualify formally but avoid leave because of income loss, career concerns, or the burden placed on colleagues.



D10 Guided

ruleを短くする時ほどexceptionが消えやすい。

“clear rules are useful” を残すなら “but…” の限定も必要。



RF05 Guided

summaryでは個別例を削り、

criteria / flexibility / practical access / qualified principle

へ抽象化する。



Model summary

Family-care leave needs clear eligibility rules, but serious care does not always occur in one predictable pattern. Policies should therefore allow limited flexibility so that different forms of care can be recognized without making administration inconsistent. Formal eligibility is also not enough, because workers may avoid leave due to financial or career concerns. Effective policy should evaluate both who qualifies and who can actually use the program, combining predictable criteria with practical and carefully limited flexibility.



Self-correction

要約の中で “all / always / any” が増えていないか確認する。原文より強い主張になっていたらexceptionを戻す。
