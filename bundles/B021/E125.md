E125｜食品寄付の安全ルール：例外条件を混合資料から判断する

P3 / LT03 / D10 Fast Introduce / SS05 / TF10

Source A

[1] A food-donation program wants supermarkets to offer unsold prepared food to local organizations rather than discard it. A simple rule would be to donate any item that remains within its stated use period at closing time.



[2] That rule is easy to understand, but time alone does not determine whether prepared food can be transferred safely. Temperature history, packaging damage, and how long an item has remained outside controlled storage may matter even when the printed date has not passed.



[3] The program therefore uses the printed date as a first screen rather than a guarantee. Food can normally be donated if the date is valid and storage conditions have remained within the approved range, unless packaging is damaged or the item has been outside controlled storage beyond the allowed period.



[4] Exceptions must be documented because otherwise staff may interpret “special cases” differently. A rejected item should be linked to an observable reason, such as a broken seal or recorded storage interruption. This makes the exception reviewable and helps the program improve training.



Notice

Pilot rule for prepared meals:

Donate items with a valid use date and verified cold storage.

Do not donate an item if its seal is broken or if recorded time outside cold storage exceeds 30 minutes.

When uncertain, place the item in the review box rather than discarding or donating it immediately.

Reading answers

Q1｜解答例

The general rule is to donate food with a valid date and verified storage. It is limited when packaging is damaged or when time outside controlled cold storage exceeds the permitted period.



Q2

The item with a valid date, intact seal, verified cold storage, and no excessive time outside storage.



Q3｜解答例

Because it prevents uncertain cases from being forced into an immediate yes/no decision and creates a record that can be reviewed for consistency.



Q4｜解答例

Because safety also depends on storage history and packaging condition, so date validity is only one screen.



D10 Transfer

災害支援とは別文脈で、

general rule → observable exceptions → qualified operating rule

へTransferする。



Model answer

The program should use a clear general rule so staff can make routine decisions consistently, but the rule must include observable exceptions for damaged packaging or unsafe storage. Exceptions should be documented because records allow later review and help identify training problems. Staff should not be free to invent exceptions without evidence, since purely discretionary decisions would make the system inconsistent and difficult to audit. Uncertain cases should be held for review rather than automatically donated or discarded.
