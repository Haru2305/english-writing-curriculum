E158｜森林回復の指標：高密度文と表から因果を読み分ける
P3 / LT03 / A29 / A28 / D04 / RQ09 / RQ13 / RF04 / SS03 / TF08
今日の狙い
高密度文を展開し、森林回復の複数指標を表と結びつける。相関する指標と原因そのものを区別する。
時間配分
Silent Plan 2分 / Reading 17分 / Dense Sentence 13分 / Table Questions 10分 / Japanese Output 10分 / Self-check 3分 / Post-solve 5分 ＝ Core 60分
Reading
[1] A restoration project planted native trees on land that had previously been used for grazing. Researchers wanted to know whether the site was recovering, but they deliberately avoided using tree count as the only measure. A young plantation can contain many trees while still lacking the structure and biological interactions of a mature forest.
[2] The team therefore monitored canopy cover, soil moisture, bird species, and seedling survival. These indicators respond at different speeds. Canopy cover may increase within a few years, while changes in bird communities can depend on whether vegetation becomes complex enough to provide food and nesting sites.
Dense Sentence
The difficulty in treating any single indicator as proof of recovery lies in the fact that an observed improvement may be both genuinely informative about one component of the system and simultaneously compatible with the continued absence of other processes—such as regeneration, habitat complexity, or species interactions—that would be expected in a more fully restored forest.
[3] This means that indicators should be read as pieces of evidence rather than interchangeable measures of the same thing. High seedling survival suggests that young trees are establishing successfully. It does not by itself show that the site supports a diverse animal community.
Table
Year | Canopy cover | Seedling survival | Bird species observed | Soil moisture
1 | 18% | 82% | 14 | Low
3 | 34% | 76% | 18 | Medium
5 | 51% | 71% | 26 | Medium
8 | 63% | 69% | 31 | Higher
[4] The table shows coordinated change, but it still does not prove that increasing canopy cover directly caused every other improvement. Rainfall varied across the study years, neighboring habitats also changed, and the restoration site had no identical untreated comparison area.
[5] The researchers therefore described the site as showing “multiple signs of recovery” rather than as fully restored. That wording preserves the positive evidence while acknowledging that ecosystem recovery is multidimensional and causal attribution remains incomplete.

解答・解説・自己修正
Q1｜解答例
One indicator can improve and still provide useful information. However, that improvement describes only one part of the system. Other recovery processes may still be absent. Therefore, one indicator alone cannot prove complete forest recovery.
Q2｜和訳例
単一の指標を回復の証拠として扱いにくいのは、ある指標の改善が生態系の一側面について確かに有益な情報を与える一方で、より十分に回復した森林で期待される再生、複雑な生息環境、種間相互作用などがなお欠けている状態とも両立しうるためである。
Q3｜解答例
Canopy cover, bird species, and soil moisture all rise over time, while seedling survival remains relatively high despite some decline.
Q4｜解答例
Because several factors changed at the same time, including rainfall and neighboring habitats, and the study lacked an identical untreated comparison area.
Q5｜解答例
The site shows several positive signs of recovery by Year 8, but the evidence does not establish complete restoration or a single causal mechanism.
Model Japanese output
森林回復は樹冠率、稚樹の定着、生物多様性、土壌状態など複数の過程から成るため、一つの改善だけでは全体を示せない。表では複数指標が改善しているが、対照区がなく他要因も変化しており、原因まで断定はできない。
## 解説の考え方

### この問題で考えること
「回復」を一つの数字へ縮めず、**複数のcomponentが別々の速度で変わるsystem**として読む。

### 考え方
canopy cover / seedling survival / bird species / soil moisture は同じものの別名ではなく、それぞれ異なる側面のindicatorである。複数列が改善することは「multiple signs of recovery」を支持するが、同時変化だけでは canopy → birds の因果は確定できない。control site がなく rainfall や neighboring habitat も変わるからである。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「樹冠率が63%まで上がったので森林は回復した」  
× 「樹冠率と鳥種数が同時に上がったので、樹冠増加が鳥を増やした」  
○ 「複数指標に改善があるので回復の兆候は強まったが、complete restoration と単一causeまでは示さない」

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
複雑なoutcomeでは、**indicator → component → overall construct** を分ける。複数指標が揃うことと、因果が証明されることも別である。


Self-correction
「同時に増えた」から「AがBを増やした」へ飛ばない。indicator / association / causation を区別する。
