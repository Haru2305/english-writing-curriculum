P3 / LT03 / SS05 / D03 / RQ13 / WF04 / WT03 / TF11

時間配分

Service Note
A town introduced a bicycle-sharing service with docking stations near a railway station, a university, a shopping street, and a residential area. During the first months, total membership grew steadily, but users often reported that bicycles were unavailable at some stations while empty return spaces were unavailable at others.
The operator first considered buying more bicycles. A closer review suggested that the larger problem was imbalance. In the morning, many bicycles moved from the residential area toward the railway station and university; by midday riders had taken them onward, leaving those stations nearly empty. In the evening, the direction partly reversed. Without redistribution, one station could become empty while another remained full.
Rules
• A bicycle must be returned to an available docking space.
• Users may check current bicycle and dock availability in the app.
• Staff redistribute bicycles twice each weekday.
• A trip cannot end at a full station.
• Users are not charged extra for choosing a different nearby station.
Table
Station | Bikes at 07:00 | Bikes at 12:00 | Empty docks at 12:00 | Failed return attempts/day
Railway | 26 | 8 | 20 | 18
University | 18 | 5 | 13 | 11
Shopping | 14 | 12 | 9 | 4
Residential | 32 | 27 | 16 | 2
User Comments
A: “I can usually find a bike near home in the morning, but the railway station is often almost empty by lunchtime.”
B: “The problem for me is returning a bike near the station when the docks are already full.”
C: “The app helps, but checking another station is inconvenient when I am already late.”
Questions

Q2｜D03

Q3｜RQ13

解答・解説・自己修正
Q1｜解答例
Bicycles and empty docks are distributed unevenly across locations and times, so users face shortages at one station and full docks at another.
Q2｜解答例
More bicycles could increase total supply without correcting where bikes and empty spaces are located. Some stations might still empty out while others become even fuller.
Q3｜解答例
The railway and university stations show the clearest evidence: bicycle availability falls sharply by midday (26 → 8 and 18 → 5), while their high numbers of failed returns (18 and 11 per day) show that the same stations also fill up at other times. The residential station still holds 27 bikes and 16 empty docks.
Model writing
The operator should increase redistribution during the late morning instead of first buying more bicycles. The table shows that the railway and university stations lose many bicycles by midday, while the railway also records frequent failed returns. User comments confirm that both shortages and full docks affect real trips. A targeted redistribution run before lunchtime could move bicycles and create empty docks where demand is concentrated. However, redistribution requires staff time and may not solve sudden demand spikes. The operator should therefore test the added run and compare failed trips before making a larger investment in bicycles or docks. This would clarify whether operations, not supply, are limiting service.
## 解説の考え方

### この問題で考えること
total bikes が足りないのか、それとも **bikeとempty dockのlocation/time imbalance** が問題なのかを切り分ける。

### 考え方
morning movement が Residential → Railway / University に偏る → destination側ではdockが埋まり、origin側ではbikeが減る → return failure と rental shortage が同時に起こり得る。ここでbuying more bikesは総量を増やしても、dock shortageを悪化させる可能性がある。したがって intervention はまず redistribution timing に当てる。

### 弱い考え方との比較
× 「自転車不足だから台数を増やす」  
○ 「where/whenの偏りでbike shortageとdock shortageが同時発生しているため、需要前に再配置する」

### 答案の組み立て
- **Position**: late-morning redistribution を追加
- **Reason**: midday imbalance が主要bottleneck
- **Mechanism**: bikeを需要側へ、empty docksを返却需要側へ同時に作る
- **Support**: Railway/Universityの減少、Railwayのfailed returns、comments
- **Qualification**: staff cost / sudden spikes
- **Return**: pilot後にfailed tripsを比較し、設備投資の必要性を判断

### 次の問題に持っていくもの
ネットワーク型サービスでは、**総量より配置と流れ**が制約になることがある。bike / beds / inventory / charging slotsでも同じ。
