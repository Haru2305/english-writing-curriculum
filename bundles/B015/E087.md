Reading
[1] Some public parks close before late evening because managers must balance access with staffing, noise, safety, and maintenance. Extending opening hours may benefit workers or students who are free only after sunset, but the effect depends on how the space is used and on the surrounding neighborhood.
[2] A useful policy question is therefore not simply “Should parks stay open later?” but “Under what conditions can later access create more benefit than cost?” A well-lit walking route may require different staffing from a large sports area. A park next to homes may need stricter noise rules than one surrounded by offices.
[3] Pilot programs can make these conditions visible. A city might extend hours on two evenings per week, limit amplified sound, and collect data on use, complaints, and operating costs. If use is high and problems remain manageable, the evidence for expansion becomes stronger. If one type of activity creates most complaints, the policy can be adjusted without abandoning later access entirely.
[4] Effective policy writing should reflect this structure. A proposal is stronger when it states a benefit, acknowledges a realistic constraint, and explains how a limited test could reduce uncertainty.

Reading answers
Q1｜解答例
The author supports evaluating later opening conditionally, using limited pilots and evidence about use, costs, noise, and safety rather than adopting a universal rule.
Q2｜解答例
The author sees pilots as useful because they reveal which conditions or activities create benefits and problems before a wider policy is adopted.
WQ04｜Effective
Effectiveは接続詞の数ではない。
claim / reason / condition / action が読み手に追えること。
一番重要な情報をどこに置くか、各文の役割が重複していないかを見る。
WP01｜Plan→Draft→Revise
構想と推敲を答案作成の外側に置かない。
短いPlanが論理へ反映され、最後に重大な問題を1つ潰すところまでが1技能。
Model answer
A city should consider extending the hours of some public parks, especially where many residents cannot use them during the day. Later access could provide more opportunities for exercise and quiet recreation. However, the policy should not be identical in every location because noise and staffing needs differ. The city could begin with two later evenings each week and monitor use, complaints, and costs. If the benefits are clear and problems remain manageable, it could then expand the program selectively. This approach would preserve access while keeping the trial easy to reverse.
Self-correction
各文の横に C/R/L/A（claim/reason/limitation/action）を付ける。役割のない文や重複する文は削るか統合する。

論証の共通回路｜ARG01–ARG06
これまでのclaim / reason / limitation / actionは別々の型ではなく、同じ論証生成の一部として考える。問いに答える→理由→なぜ成り立つか→具体化→必要なら限定→問いへ戻る。すべてを毎回使う必要はない
